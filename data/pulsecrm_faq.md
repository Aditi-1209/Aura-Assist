# PulseCRM Help Center — Frequently Asked Questions

## Account & Billing

**Q: What pricing plans does PulseCRM offer?**
PulseCRM offers three plans: Starter, Growth, and Enterprise. Starter is designed for small teams and includes core contact records and deal pipelines, Growth adds email sequences and sales forecasting, and Enterprise includes advanced permissions, custom reporting, and a dedicated account manager. You can compare full plan details on the Billing page inside your account settings.

**Q: How do I upgrade from the Starter plan to Growth?**
Go to Settings > Billing > Change Plan, select Growth, and confirm the upgrade. Your new features, including email sequences and sales forecasting, are unlocked immediately, and you'll be billed a prorated amount for the remainder of your current billing cycle. No data is lost during the upgrade.

**Q: I forgot my password, what should I do?**
Click "Forgot password?" on the login screen and enter the email address associated with your PulseCRM account. We'll send a secure reset link that expires after 60 minutes. If you don't receive the email within a few minutes, check your spam folder or contact support@pulsecrm.com.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
You can reset your password from the login page by selecting "Forgot password?" and following the emailed instructions, or from within the app under Settings > Security > Change Password if you're already logged in. For security reasons, password reset links expire after 60 minutes.

**Q: Can I cancel my subscription at any time?**
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time from Settings > Billing > Manage Subscription. Your account will remain active until the end of your current billing period, after which it will downgrade to a read-only state for 30 days before deletion. There are no cancellation fees on any plan.

**Q: What happens to my data if I cancel my account?**
After cancellation, your account enters a 30-day read-only grace period during which you can export your contact records, deal pipelines, and reports. After 30 days, all data is permanently deleted from our production systems in accordance with our data retention policy. We recommend exporting a full backup before the grace period ends.

**Q: Do you offer discounts for annual billing?**
Yes, switching from monthly to annual billing on the Growth or Enterprise plan saves you the equivalent of two months per year. You can switch billing frequency anytime from Settings > Billing > Payment Cycle, and the discount is applied automatically on your next invoice.

**Q: How many users can I add on the Growth plan?**
The Growth plan supports up to 20 users per account. If you need more seats, you can add them individually for a per-seat fee or upgrade to the Enterprise plan, which includes unlimited users along with advanced permissions and custom reporting.

**Q: Where can I find my past invoices?**
All invoices are available under Settings > Billing > Invoice History, where you can view, download, or email a PDF copy of any past invoice. Invoices are also automatically emailed to your billing contact on the date of each charge.

**Q: My credit card payment failed, what now?**
If a payment fails, PulseCRM will automatically retry the charge three times over seven days and notify your billing contact by email after each attempt. You can update your card details at any time under Settings > Billing > Payment Method to avoid service interruption. If all retries fail, your account moves to a grace period before features are restricted.

## Setup & Installation

**Q: How do I create my PulseCRM account?**
Visit pulsecrm.com/signup, enter your work email and company name, and verify your email address using the link we send you. Once verified, you'll be guided through a short onboarding wizard that helps you import contacts and set up your first deal pipeline.

**Q: What are the system requirements for using PulseCRM?**
PulseCRM is a cloud-based application that runs in any modern browser, including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge, with no software installation required. For the best experience, we recommend keeping your browser updated to the latest version and enabling JavaScript and cookies.

**Q: How do I import my existing contacts into PulseCRM?**
Go to Contacts > Import, then upload a CSV file or connect directly to Gmail or Outlook to sync your existing contacts automatically. PulseCRM will map your spreadsheet columns to contact record fields, and you can review the mapping before confirming the import.

**Q: Is there a mobile app for PulseCRM?**
Yes, PulseCRM offers native mobile apps for iOS and Android, available on the App Store and Google Play. The mobile app supports contact records, deal pipelines, and notifications, though some advanced reporting features are currently desktop-only.

**Q: How do I set up my first deal pipeline?**
Navigate to Pipelines > Create New Pipeline, name your pipeline, and define the stages your deals move through, such as Lead, Qualified, Proposal, and Closed Won. You can customize stage names and add as many pipelines as you need on the Growth and Enterprise plans.

**Q: How do I invite team members to my account?**
Go to Settings > Team > Invite Members, enter their email addresses, and assign a role such as Admin, Sales Rep, or Viewer. Invited users will receive an email with a link to set up their password and join your account.

**Q: Can I customize the fields on my contact records?**
Yes, custom fields can be added to contact records under Settings > Customization > Contact Fields. You can create text, dropdown, date, and number fields to capture information specific to your business, and these fields appear automatically in filters and reports.

**Q: How do I migrate data from another CRM into PulseCRM?**
PulseCRM supports CSV imports for contact records and deal pipelines, and our migration team can assist Enterprise customers with custom data migrations from platforms like Salesforce or HubSpot. Contact support@pulsecrm.com to request a guided migration session.

**Q: I just signed up, how do I set up my account from scratch?**
After verifying your email, the onboarding wizard will walk you through creating your first deal pipeline, importing contacts via CSV or Gmail/Outlook sync, and inviting your team. You can revisit any onboarding step later from Settings > Getting Started.

**Q: How do I set up email sequences for new leads?**
Go to Sequences > Create Sequence, choose a trigger such as "New Contact Added," and build a series of timed emails with delays between each step. Email sequences are available on the Growth and Enterprise plans and can be paused or stopped automatically when a contact replies.

## Features & Usage

**Q: What is a deal pipeline in PulseCRM?**
A deal pipeline is a visual representation of your sales process, organized into stages such as Lead, Qualified, Proposal, and Closed Won. You can drag and drop deals between stages, track deal value, and set close dates, giving your team a clear view of where every opportunity stands.

**Q: How does sales forecasting work in PulseCRM?**
Sales forecasting analyzes the deals in your pipelines, weighting them by stage and historical close rates, to project expected revenue over a given period. Forecasts are available on the Growth and Enterprise plans and can be filtered by team, pipeline, or individual sales rep.

**Q: What information is stored in a contact record?**
A contact record stores a person's name, email, phone number, company, custom fields you've defined, and a full activity timeline including emails, calls, notes, and associated deals. Contact records are the central hub for all customer relationship data in PulseCRM.

**Q: How do email sequences work?**
Email sequences let you automate a series of follow-up emails sent to contacts based on a trigger, such as joining a pipeline stage or filling out a form. Each step can include a delay, and sequences automatically pause if the contact replies or unsubscribes.

**Q: Can I generate custom reports in PulseCRM?**
Yes, custom reporting is available on the Enterprise plan and lets you build reports using any combination of contact record fields, deal pipeline stages, and activity data. Reports can be scheduled to email automatically to your team on a daily, weekly, or monthly basis.

**Q: How do I move a deal to a different pipeline stage?**
You can drag and drop the deal card to the new stage directly on the Pipelines view, or open the deal record and select the new stage from the dropdown menu. Stage changes are logged automatically in the deal's activity timeline.

**Q: Does PulseCRM support task reminders and follow-ups?**
Yes, you can create tasks tied to any contact record or deal, set a due date, and assign it to a team member. PulseCRM sends reminder notifications by email and within the app dashboard as the due date approaches.

**Q: How can I see all the activity history for a specific contact?**
Open the contact record and scroll to the Activity Timeline section, which displays every email, call log, note, and deal associated with that contact in chronological order. You can filter the timeline by activity type using the dropdown at the top of the section.

**Q: What's the difference between a Sales Rep role and an Admin role?**
A Sales Rep can manage their own contact records, deal pipelines, and email sequences, but cannot change account-wide settings or billing. An Admin has full access, including team management, billing, custom field configuration, and integration setup.

**Q: Can multiple team members work on the same deal pipeline?**
Yes, deal pipelines are shared across your team by default, and Admins can configure visibility rules under Settings > Team > Permissions to restrict certain pipelines to specific roles. All changes to a shared pipeline are visible in real time to everyone with access.

## Troubleshooting

**Q: PulseCRM is loading slowly, what can I do?**
First, try clearing your browser cache and confirming you're on the latest version of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge. If the issue persists, check our status page at status.pulsecrm.com for any ongoing incidents, or contact support with details about your browser and operating system.

**Q: Why am I not receiving email notifications from PulseCRM?**
Check Settings > Notifications to confirm email alerts are enabled for the relevant activity types, and verify that emails from notifications@pulsecrm.com are not being filtered to spam. If you recently connected Gmail or Outlook, also confirm the sync permissions were granted during setup.

**Q: My CSV import failed, why?**
The most common cause is a mismatch between your column headers and the required contact record fields, or special characters in the file encoding. Re-export your file as UTF-8 CSV, double-check the column mapping step, and try the import again; if it still fails, contact support with the file attached.

**Q: I can't log into my account, what should I do?**
First, confirm you're using the correct email and try the "Forgot password?" link to reset your credentials. If you still can't access your account after resetting your password, contact support@pulsecrm.com and we'll verify your identity and restore access.

**Q: Why is my Gmail sync not working?**
Gmail sync issues are usually caused by a revoked or expired authorization token. Go to Settings > Integrations > Gmail, click Reconnect, and re-approve the requested permissions. If the problem continues, check that your Google Workspace admin hasn't blocked third-party app access.

**Q: Why aren't my email sequences sending?**
Check that the sequence status is set to Active rather than Paused, and confirm the triggering condition, such as a pipeline stage change, is actually being met by your contacts. Also verify your connected email account under Settings > Integrations hasn't lost its authorization, as this will silently pause outgoing sequence emails.

**Q: Deals are missing from my pipeline view, where did they go?**
Check whether a filter is currently applied to the Pipelines view, as active filters by owner, date range, or tag can hide deals that don't match the criteria. You can clear all filters using the "Reset Filters" button at the top of the page to confirm the deals are still present.

**Q: I'm getting an error when trying to add a new contact.**
This error usually occurs when a required custom field is left blank or when a duplicate email address already exists in another contact record. Review the form for any fields marked with a red asterisk and check Contacts > Duplicates to see if a matching record already exists.

**Q: Why did my Zapier automation stop working?**
Zapier automations typically stop when the connected PulseCRM API key has been regenerated or revoked. Go to Settings > Integrations > Zapier, generate a new API key if needed, and update the connection in your Zap's PulseCRM trigger or action step.

**Q: The mobile app won't sync with my desktop account.**
Confirm you're logged into the mobile app with the same email address used on desktop, and check that your device has an active internet connection. If data still won't sync, try logging out and back into the mobile app, which forces a full resync of contact records and deal pipelines.

## Integrations

**Q: What integrations does PulseCRM support?**
PulseCRM integrates natively with Gmail, Outlook, Slack, and Zapier, allowing you to sync email, receive notifications, and connect to thousands of other apps. Enterprise customers also get access to our REST API for custom integrations.

**Q: How do I connect my Gmail account to PulseCRM?**
Go to Settings > Integrations > Gmail and click Connect, then sign in with your Google account and approve the requested permissions. Once connected, emails sent to and from your contacts will automatically appear in their contact record's activity timeline.

**Q: How do I connect Outlook to PulseCRM?**
Navigate to Settings > Integrations > Outlook, click Connect, and authenticate with your Microsoft 365 or Outlook.com credentials. After connecting, your sent and received emails will sync automatically with matching contact records, just like with Gmail.

**Q: Can I get PulseCRM notifications in Slack?**
Yes, connect Slack under Settings > Integrations > Slack and choose which channel should receive notifications for events like new deals, stage changes, or task reminders. You can customize which notification types are sent to Slack from the same settings page.

**Q: How does the Zapier integration work?**
The Zapier integration lets you connect PulseCRM to thousands of other apps using triggers like "New Contact Created" or "Deal Stage Changed," paired with actions in tools such as Google Sheets or Mailchimp. You'll need an active Zapier account and a PulseCRM API key, available under Settings > Integrations > Zapier.

**Q: Does PulseCRM have a public API?**
Yes, PulseCRM offers a REST API for Enterprise customers, allowing programmatic access to contact records, deal pipelines, and activity data. Full API documentation, including authentication and rate limits, is available at developers.pulsecrm.com.

**Q: Can I integrate PulseCRM with my company's custom internal tools?**
Enterprise customers can use the PulseCRM REST API to build custom integrations with internal tools, or use Zapier for lighter-weight, no-code connections. Our solutions team can also advise on integration architecture during onboarding.

**Q: I disconnected Gmail by accident, how do I reconnect it?**
Go to Settings > Integrations > Gmail and click Connect again, then sign in and re-approve the permissions. Previously synced emails remain in your contact records' activity timelines, and new emails will resume syncing immediately after reconnecting.

**Q: Does connecting Outlook or Gmail give PulseCRM access to all my emails?**
No, PulseCRM only syncs email threads involving addresses that match an existing contact record in your account. We do not store or scan emails unrelated to your CRM contacts, and you can review exactly what's synced under Settings > Integrations > Sync Activity.

**Q: How do I find my PulseCRM API key for Zapier or custom integrations?**
Your API key is available under Settings > Integrations > API Keys, where Admins can generate, view, or revoke keys. We recommend rotating your API key periodically and revoking any keys that are no longer in use.

## Security & Privacy

**Q: How does PulseCRM protect my data?**
PulseCRM encrypts data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and encrypts data at rest using AES-256. Our infrastructure runs on SOC 2 Type II certified data centers, and we conduct regular third-party security audits and penetration testing.

**Q: Does PulseCRM support two-factor authentication?**
Yes, two-factor authentication can be enabled under Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication using an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or Authy. We strongly recommend enabling it for all users, and Enterprise admins can enforce it account-wide.

**Q: Who can access my company's contact records and deal pipelines?**
Access is controlled by the roles assigned to each team member, such as Admin, Sales Rep, or Viewer, and Admins can further restrict pipeline visibility under Settings > Team > Permissions. PulseCRM staff only access customer data with explicit permission for support purposes.

**Q: Is PulseCRM compliant with GDPR?**
Yes, PulseCRM is fully GDPR compliant and provides tools for data export, rectification, and deletion requests under Settings > Privacy > Data Requests. We also offer a Data Processing Addendum (DPA) for customers operating in the EU, available on request.

**Q: How do I enable two-factor authentication on my account?**
Go to Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication, scan the QR code with an authenticator app like Google Authenticator or Authy, and enter the generated code to confirm setup. You'll be prompted for a code at login from then on, in addition to your password.

**Q: Where is PulseCRM customer data hosted?**
Customer data is hosted in SOC 2 Type II certified data centers located in the United States and the European Union, with EU customers able to request EU-only data residency on the Enterprise plan. Backups are encrypted and geographically redundant.

**Q: Can I export all my data if I want to leave PulseCRM?**
Yes, you can export your contact records, deal pipelines, and activity history at any time from Settings > Data > Export, in CSV or JSON format. This is also available automatically during the 30-day grace period after canceling your subscription.

**Q: What happens to my data if I delete my account?**
Once you confirm account deletion, all contact records, deal pipelines, and associated data are permanently removed from our production systems within 30 days, with backups purged on a rolling 90-day cycle thereafter. This action cannot be undone, so we recommend exporting your data first.

**Q: Does PulseCRM sell or share my data with third parties?**
No, PulseCRM does not sell customer data to third parties under any circumstances. Data is only shared with the integrations you explicitly connect, such as Gmail, Outlook, Slack, or Zapier, and solely to provide the functionality you've enabled.

**Q: How do I report a security vulnerability or suspicious activity?**
You can report security concerns directly to security@pulsecrm.com, and our security team will respond within 24 hours. For suspicious account activity, such as an unrecognized login, we also recommend immediately changing your password and enabling two-factor authentication.
