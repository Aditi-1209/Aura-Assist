# InvoicePilot Help Center — Frequently Asked Questions

## Account & Billing

**Q: How do I create an InvoicePilot account?**
Visit invoicepilot.com/signup and enter your business email address and a password to get started. You'll be asked to verify your email before accessing your dashboard, after which you can choose between the Starter, Growth, and Pro plans. A 14-day free trial of the Growth plan is automatically applied to all new accounts.

**Q: What is the difference between the Starter, Growth, and Pro plans?**
The Starter plan ($15/month) includes up to 25 invoices per month and basic invoice templates. The Growth plan ($39/month) adds Recurring Invoices, Smart Reminders, and unlimited invoicing for up to 3 team members. The Pro plan ($89/month) unlocks Multi-Currency Mode, the Client Portal, and priority support for larger teams.

**Q: How do I upgrade or downgrade my subscription?**
Go to Settings > Billing and select "Change Plan." Upgrades take effect immediately and you'll be billed a prorated amount for the remainder of your cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle so you keep access to your current features until then.

**Q: I forgot my password, what should I do?**
Click "Forgot password?" on the login screen and enter the email address associated with your account. We'll send a secure reset link that expires after 60 minutes for your protection. If you don't see the email within a few minutes, check your spam folder before requesting a new link.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
Navigate to the login page and select "Forgot password?" beneath the sign-in form. Enter your account email and follow the link sent to your inbox to set a new password. For security reasons, you'll be logged out of all active sessions once the reset is complete.

**Q: Can I cancel my subscription at any time?**
Yes, you can cancel anytime from Settings > Billing > "Cancel Subscription" with no cancellation fees. Your account will remain active until the end of your current billing period, after which it converts to a read-only Free view. You can reactivate at any point and pick up where you left off.

**Q: Does InvoicePilot offer refunds?**
We offer a full refund within 14 days of your initial purchase if you're not satisfied, no questions asked. Refund requests after that window are evaluated on a case-by-case basis by our billing team. To request a refund, contact support@invoicepilot.com with your account email and invoice number.

**Q: How do I update my billing information or credit card on file?**
Go to Settings > Billing > Payment Method and click "Update Card." InvoicePilot processes all subscription payments securely through Stripe, so your card details are never stored on our servers. Changes take effect immediately for your next billing cycle.

**Q: Can I add multiple team members to one account?**
Yes, the Growth plan supports up to 3 team members and the Pro plan supports up to 10, each with their own login and permission level. Additional seats beyond your plan limit can be purchased for $8/month per user. Team management is available under Settings > Team.

**Q: Where can I find my past invoices and receipts for my InvoicePilot subscription?**
All of your subscription receipts are available under Settings > Billing > Billing History. You can download any past receipt as a PDF for your records or accounting purposes. Receipts are generated automatically on the day your payment is processed.

## Setup & Installation

**Q: How do I get started with InvoicePilot after signing up?**
Once you've verified your email, the Setup Wizard will guide you through adding your business details, logo, and tax information. From there, you can import existing clients or add them manually before creating your first invoice. Most users are ready to send their first invoice within ten minutes.

**Q: Is there a desktop app for InvoicePilot, or is it web-only?**
InvoicePilot is primarily a web-based platform accessible from any modern browser, with no desktop installation required. For on-the-go access, we offer InvoicePilot Mobile for iOS and Android, which syncs in real time with your web account. There is currently no standalone desktop application.

**Q: How do I install InvoicePilot Mobile?**
Search for "InvoicePilot" in the Apple App Store or Google Play Store and download the free app. Log in using your existing InvoicePilot credentials to sync your invoices, clients, and Expense Tracker data automatically. Push notifications for Smart Reminders can be enabled during the first launch.

**Q: How do I add my company logo and branding to invoices?**
Go to Settings > Business Profile and upload your logo in PNG or JPG format, ideally at least 300x300 pixels. You can also customize your invoice accent color and footer text from the same screen. These branding settings apply automatically to all future invoices and the Client Portal.

**Q: How do I set up my tax rates?**
Navigate to Settings > Taxes and click "Add Tax Rate" to define rates by name, percentage, and region. You can apply default tax rates per client or override them on individual invoices as needed. InvoicePilot supports multiple simultaneous tax rates for businesses operating across regions.

**Q: Can I import my existing clients and invoices from another platform?**
Yes, go to Settings > Import and upload a CSV file of your client list and historical invoices using our provided template. InvoicePilot also supports direct migration from QuickBooks Online and Xero through the Integrations tab. Most imports complete within a few minutes depending on file size.

**Q: How do I set up automatic recurring invoices?**
Open the Recurring Invoices tab from your dashboard and click "New Recurring Invoice." Choose the client, line items, and billing frequency (weekly, monthly, or custom), and InvoicePilot will generate and send invoices automatically on schedule. You'll receive a notification each time a recurring invoice is issued.

**Q: What do I need to do to start accepting online payments?**
Connect a payment processor under Settings > Integrations > Payments, choosing either Stripe or PayPal. Once connected, a "Pay Now" button will automatically appear on all outgoing invoices. Funds are deposited directly into your connected Stripe or PayPal account according to their standard payout schedule.

**Q: How do I set up multiple currencies for international clients?**
Multi-Currency Mode is available on the Pro plan and can be enabled under Settings > Preferences > Currency. Once enabled, you can assign a default currency per client and InvoicePilot will handle exchange rate conversion automatically using daily market rates. Reports can be viewed in your home currency or the original invoice currency.

**Q: How do I invite my accountant or bookkeeper to my account?**
Go to Settings > Team > Invite Member and select the "Accountant" role, which grants reporting and export access without allowing invoice edits. Enter their email address and they'll receive an invitation to create their own login. This role does not count against your plan's team member limit.

## Features & Usage

**Q: How do I create and send my first invoice?**
From your dashboard, click "New Invoice," select or add a client, and enter your line items, quantities, and rates. Review the preview, then click "Send" to email it directly to your client with a secure payment link attached. You can also save it as a draft if you're not ready to send.

**Q: What is the Client Portal and how does it work?**
The Client Portal is a secure, branded webpage where your clients can view, download, and pay all of their invoices in one place. It's available on the Pro plan and is enabled by default once activated under Settings > Client Portal. Clients access it via a unique link sent in their invoice emails, with no separate account required.

**Q: How do Smart Reminders work?**
Smart Reminders automatically email clients about upcoming or overdue invoices based on rules you configure, such as 3 days before and 7 days after the due date. You can customize the reminder schedule, tone, and message template under Settings > Reminders. Reminder activity is logged on each invoice's timeline for your reference.

**Q: Can I track business expenses in InvoicePilot?**
Yes, the Expense Tracker lets you log expenses manually or by uploading a receipt photo, which InvoicePilot scans to extract the amount and vendor automatically. Expenses can be categorized and optionally marked as billable to a specific client. Billable expenses can then be added directly to an invoice with one click.

**Q: How do I create a recurring invoice for a retainer client?**
Use the Recurring Invoices feature found on your dashboard sidebar. Set the billing frequency to match your retainer agreement, such as monthly, and InvoicePilot will automatically generate, send, and track payment for each cycle. You can pause or edit the recurring schedule at any time without affecting past invoices.

**Q: What file formats can I export invoices and reports in?**
Invoices can be downloaded as PDF files directly from the invoice view screen. Reports, including the Profit & Loss summary and client statements, can be exported as PDF or CSV from the Reports tab. CSV exports are commonly used for importing data into spreadsheet tools or external accounting software.

**Q: How do I set up partial payments or payment plans on an invoice?**
When creating an invoice, toggle "Allow Partial Payments" in the payment settings section before sending. Clients paying through the Client Portal or a Stripe/PayPal payment link can then choose to pay any amount up to the total due. The invoice will show as "Partially Paid" until the full balance is settled.

**Q: Can I customize my invoice templates?**
Yes, go to Settings > Templates to choose from several layout styles and customize fonts, colors, and field visibility. You can also save multiple templates for different purposes, such as one for retainers and another for one-off projects. Your default template is applied automatically to new invoices unless you select a different one.

**Q: How do I generate financial reports for my business?**
Open the Reports tab to access pre-built reports including Profit & Loss, Accounts Receivable Aging, and Client Revenue summaries. You can filter any report by date range, client, or currency before exporting it as a PDF or CSV. Reports update in real time as new invoices and payments are recorded.

**Q: How do I add discounts or late fees to an invoice?**
While editing an invoice, click "Add Discount" to apply either a flat amount or percentage off the subtotal. Late fees can be configured globally under Settings > Late Fees, where you can set automatic fees for invoices that pass their due date. Both adjustments appear as separate line items for transparency.

## Troubleshooting

**Q: My invoice email isn't being received by my client. What should I check?**
First, confirm the client's email address is entered correctly under their contact details. Ask your client to check their spam or junk folder, since some email providers flag automated invoice emails. You can also resend the invoice manually or copy the payment link to send through another channel.

**Q: Why is my payment showing as pending after my client paid through Stripe?**
Stripe payments typically clear within a few minutes, but bank-funded payments can take up to 5 business days to fully process. The invoice will automatically update to "Paid" once Stripe confirms settlement on their end. If the pending status persists beyond a week, contact support with the transaction ID.

**Q: I can't log in to my account even though I'm using the correct password.**
First, make sure Caps Lock is off and that you're entering the email address tied to your InvoicePilot account. If you have two-factor authentication enabled, confirm your authenticator app's time is synced correctly, as expired codes are a common cause of failed logins. If the issue continues, use the "Forgot password?" link to reset your credentials.

**Q: Why isn't my recurring invoice sending automatically?**
Check that the recurring invoice status under the Recurring Invoices tab is set to "Active" and not "Paused." Also verify that your client's email address hasn't bounced previously, as InvoicePilot pauses delivery to addresses with repeated bounces. You can manually trigger the next cycle by clicking "Send Now" if needed.

**Q: My QuickBooks Online sync isn't updating my latest invoices.**
Go to Settings > Integrations > QuickBooks Online and check the "Last Synced" timestamp to confirm the connection is active. Sync issues are often caused by an expired authorization token, which can be refreshed by clicking "Reconnect." If invoices still don't appear after reconnecting, try a manual sync from the same screen.

**Q: The PDF I downloaded looks different from what I see on screen.**
This is usually caused by a custom font or logo that hasn't fully loaded before the PDF was generated. Try refreshing the invoice page and waiting a few seconds before downloading again. If the issue persists, switch to a standard template under Settings > Templates and regenerate the PDF.

**Q: Why are my tax calculations on an invoice incorrect?**
Check Settings > Taxes to confirm the correct tax rate is assigned as default for that client or region. It's also worth checking whether a line item was accidentally marked as tax-exempt during invoice creation. Editing the tax rate on an existing draft invoice will automatically recalculate the total.

**Q: My Expense Tracker receipt scan isn't reading the amount correctly.**
Receipt scanning works best with clear, well-lit photos where the total amount is clearly visible and not cut off. If the scan misreads a value, you can manually correct the amount, vendor, or date before saving the expense. Blurry or faded receipts may need to be entered manually rather than scanned.

**Q: I'm getting an error when trying to send an invoice. What should I do?**
Most send errors occur when a required field, such as a client email or invoice due date, is left blank. Double-check that all mandatory fields are filled in and that your internet connection is stable. If the error message persists after these checks, take a screenshot and contact support@invoicepilot.com with your invoice number.

**Q: Why did my client's payment fail when using the Client Portal?**
Payment failures are most often caused by an expired card or insufficient funds on the client's end, both of which are reported directly by Stripe or PayPal. The client portal will display the specific decline reason returned by the payment processor when available. Encourage your client to try an alternate payment method or contact their bank.

## Integrations

**Q: Does InvoicePilot integrate with QuickBooks Online?**
Yes, InvoicePilot offers a two-way sync with QuickBooks Online that keeps your invoices, payments, and client records aligned across both platforms. You can enable this under Settings > Integrations > QuickBooks Online by logging in with your QuickBooks credentials. Syncing typically occurs automatically every 15 minutes.

**Q: Can I connect InvoicePilot to Xero?**
Yes, Xero integration is available under Settings > Integrations > Xero and supports syncing invoices, contacts, and payment records. This integration is available on the Growth and Pro plans. Note that you can connect to either QuickBooks Online or Xero at one time, not both simultaneously.

**Q: How do I connect Stripe to accept credit card payments?**
Go to Settings > Integrations > Payments and click "Connect" next to Stripe, then follow the prompts to link or create a Stripe account. Once connected, a secure "Pay Now" button is automatically added to all invoices and the Client Portal. Stripe's standard processing fees apply per transaction and are charged directly by Stripe.

**Q: Is PayPal supported as a payment option for clients?**
Yes, PayPal can be connected alongside or instead of Stripe under Settings > Integrations > Payments. Clients will see both available payment options at checkout if you have both connected. PayPal payouts follow PayPal's own standard transfer timelines, separate from InvoicePilot.

**Q: Does InvoicePilot have a Zapier integration?**
Yes, InvoicePilot connects with Zapier, allowing you to automate workflows like creating invoices from form submissions or adding new clients to a CRM. You can find the InvoicePilot app in the Zapier directory and connect it using an API key generated under Settings > Integrations > API. Zapier integration is available on the Growth and Pro plans.

**Q: Can I use InvoicePilot's API to build a custom integration?**
Yes, our REST API allows you to programmatically create invoices, manage clients, and retrieve payment data. API keys can be generated under Settings > Integrations > API, and full documentation is available at developers.invoicepilot.com. API access is included with the Growth and Pro plans.

**Q: How do I switch from QuickBooks Online to Xero within InvoicePilot?**
Go to Settings > Integrations, disconnect QuickBooks Online first, and confirm you want to stop syncing. Once disconnected, you can connect Xero from the same screen and run an initial sync to bring in your existing data. Historical sync records from QuickBooks Online remain in your account but will no longer update.

**Q: Will disconnecting an accounting integration delete my invoice data?**
No, disconnecting QuickBooks Online or Xero only stops future syncing and does not delete any invoices, clients, or payment records already in InvoicePilot. Your data remains fully accessible and exportable at any time regardless of integration status. You can reconnect the same integration later without losing historical records.

**Q: Can I import my contact list from another CRM?**
Yes, you can import contacts via CSV under Settings > Import, or use our Zapier integration to automatically sync new contacts from supported CRMs. Imported contacts are automatically checked for duplicates based on email address. Imported clients retain any existing invoice history once linked via QuickBooks Online or Xero.

**Q: Does InvoicePilot support bank account syncing for reconciliation?**
Bank syncing is available through our QuickBooks Online and Xero integrations, which both support direct bank feed reconciliation on their platforms. InvoicePilot itself focuses on invoicing and payment tracking rather than direct bank connections. Payments received via Stripe or PayPal are automatically matched to the correct invoice for easier reconciliation.

## Security & Privacy

**Q: How does InvoicePilot protect my financial data?**
All data is encrypted both in transit and at rest using InvoicePilot Vault, our dedicated encryption layer built on industry-standard AES-256 encryption. Access to production systems is restricted to authorized personnel and logged for auditing purposes. We also undergo regular third-party security assessments to validate our safeguards.

**Q: Does InvoicePilot support two-factor authentication?**
Yes, two-factor authentication (2FA) can be enabled under Settings > Security using any standard authenticator app, such as Google Authenticator or Authy. We strongly recommend enabling 2FA for all accounts, especially those with billing or admin permissions. Once enabled, you'll be prompted for a verification code at each new login.

**Q: How do I enable two-factor authentication on my account?**
Go to Settings > Security and click "Enable Two-Factor Authentication." Scan the QR code with your authenticator app and enter the generated code to confirm setup. You'll also receive a set of backup codes, which you should store somewhere safe in case you lose access to your authenticator app.

**Q: Who can access my client and invoice data within my organization?**
Access is controlled through role-based permissions set under Settings > Team, where each team member is assigned a role such as Admin, Member, or Accountant. Admins have full access, while other roles have restricted visibility into billing or sensitive settings. All account activity is logged and viewable under Settings > Activity Log.

**Q: Is my payment information stored on InvoicePilot's servers?**
No, InvoicePilot never stores full credit card numbers; all payment processing and card storage is handled directly by Stripe or PayPal, both of which are PCI-DSS compliant. InvoicePilot only retains transaction metadata, such as payment status and amount, for record-keeping. This separation reduces risk and keeps sensitive card data out of our systems entirely.

**Q: Does InvoicePilot comply with GDPR?**
Yes, InvoicePilot is fully GDPR-compliant and provides tools to help you manage client data in accordance with the regulation, including data export and deletion requests. Our Data Processing Agreement is available upon request from support@invoicepilot.com. EU customer data is stored in EU-based data centers to meet residency requirements.

**Q: How do I delete my account and all associated data?**
Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account and confirm your decision, which is irreversible after a 30-day grace period. During the grace period, you can log back in to cancel the deletion request. After 30 days, all invoices, client data, and account information are permanently erased from InvoicePilot Vault.

**Q: What happens to my data if I cancel my subscription instead of deleting my account?**
Canceling your subscription does not delete your data; your account simply converts to a read-only Free view where you can still view and export past invoices. Your data remains securely stored under InvoicePilot Vault until you choose to delete the account entirely. You can reactivate a paid plan at any time to regain full functionality.

**Q: Does InvoicePilot sell or share my data with third parties?**
No, InvoicePilot does not sell client or business data to third parties under any circumstances. Data is only shared with the integrations and payment processors you explicitly connect, such as Stripe, PayPal, QuickBooks Online, or Xero. Full details are outlined in our Privacy Policy at invoicepilot.com/privacy.

**Q: How can I see who has logged into my account recently?**
Go to Settings > Security > Login History to view a list of recent sign-ins, including device type, approximate location, and timestamp. If you notice any unfamiliar activity, you can immediately revoke all active sessions and reset your password from the same screen. We also recommend enabling two-factor authentication for additional protection going forward.
