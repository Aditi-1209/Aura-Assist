# TaskFlow Help Center — Frequently Asked Questions

## Account & Billing

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
Go to the login page and click "Forgot password?" to receive a reset link at your registered email address. The link is valid for 60 minutes, after which you'll need to request a new one. If you don't see the email, check your spam folder before contacting support.

**Q: I forgot my password — what should I do?**
You can reset it yourself from the login screen by selecting "Forgot password?" and following the emailed instructions. No need to contact support unless the reset email doesn't arrive within a few minutes. Make sure you're checking the inbox tied to your TaskFlow account.

**Q: What pricing plans does TaskFlow offer?**
TaskFlow offers four plans: Free, Starter ($8/user/month), Pro ($15/user/month), and Enterprise (custom pricing). Each tier unlocks additional features such as automations, custom fields, and advanced reporting. You can compare plan details on the Pricing page within your account settings.

**Q: How do I upgrade from the Free plan to Pro?**
Open Workspace Settings, select "Billing," and choose the Pro plan from the available options. You'll be prompted to enter payment details and your new features will be active immediately after confirmation. Billing is prorated for the remainder of your current cycle.

**Q: Can I cancel my subscription at any time?**
Yes, you can cancel anytime from Workspace Settings > Billing > Manage Subscription. Your plan will remain active until the end of the current billing period, after which your workspace reverts to the Free plan. No cancellation fees apply.

**Q: Why was my credit card declined?**
Card declines are usually caused by insufficient funds, an expired card, or a mismatch in billing address details. Double-check your payment information under Billing Settings and try again, or contact your card issuer if the issue persists. TaskFlow does not store full card numbers, so we cannot diagnose issuer-side declines directly.

**Q: How do I add or remove team members from billing?**
Under Workspace Settings > Members, you can invite or remove users, and your invoice will automatically adjust to reflect the active seat count. Removed members are deactivated immediately, and any unused prorated cost is credited to your next invoice. Only Workspace Admins can manage billing-related seat changes.

**Q: Where can I download my invoices?**
Invoices are available under Workspace Settings > Billing > Invoice History, where you can download PDF copies for any past billing cycle. Invoices are also emailed automatically to your billing contact each month. If you need a custom invoice format, contact our billing team.

**Q: Does TaskFlow offer a discount for nonprofits or students?**
Yes, TaskFlow offers a 50% discount on Pro plans for verified nonprofits and educational institutions. Submit a verification request through the Billing page, and our team will review it within 2-3 business days. Approved discounts are applied automatically to future invoices.

**Q: What happens to my data if I downgrade my plan?**
Your data is preserved when downgrading, but features exclusive to higher tiers — such as automations or custom fields — become read-only until you upgrade again. No boards, tasks, or files are deleted as a result of a plan change. You'll receive an email summary of any features affected before the downgrade takes effect.

## Setup & Installation

**Q: How do I create my first workspace?**
After signing up, click "Create Workspace" and enter a name, industry, and team size. TaskFlow will generate a sample board to help you get oriented, which you can delete once you're comfortable with the interface. You can create additional workspaces later from the workspace switcher.

**Q: Is there a desktop app for TaskFlow?**
Yes, TaskFlow offers native desktop apps for Mac, Windows, and Linux, downloadable from the Apps page in your account settings. The desktop app supports offline access and native notifications. It syncs automatically with the web version once you're back online.

**Q: How do I install the TaskFlow mobile app?**
Search for "TaskFlow" in the Apple App Store or Google Play Store and install it like any other app. Log in with your existing credentials, and your boards and tasks will sync automatically. Push notifications can be configured in the app's settings menu.

**Q: How do I invite teammates to my workspace?**
Go to Workspace Settings > Members > Invite, then enter email addresses or share the workspace invite link. Invited users will receive an email prompting them to create an account or log in if they already have one. You can set their role (Admin, Member, or Guest) at the time of invitation.

**Q: Can I import tasks from another tool like Trello or Asana?**
Yes, TaskFlow supports CSV import as well as direct importers for Trello and Asana, found under Workspace Settings > Import. The importer maps boards, lists, and cards to TaskFlow's equivalent structures automatically. We recommend reviewing the mapped data before finalizing the import.

**Q: How do I set up my first project board?**
Click "New Board" from your workspace dashboard and choose a template — such as Kanban, Scrum, or Blank. Add columns to represent your workflow stages, then start creating tasks within them. Boards can be customized further at any time using the Board Settings menu.

**Q: What are workspaces and how are they different from boards?**
A workspace is the top-level container for your team, organization, or department, while boards live inside a workspace and represent individual projects. You can have multiple boards within one workspace, each with its own members and settings. Workspaces are useful for separating clients, departments, or business units.

**Q: How do I set up two-factor authentication during onboarding?**
During account setup, you'll be prompted to enable two-factor authentication (2FA) using an authenticator app or SMS. You can skip this step initially, but we strongly recommend enabling it from Account Settings > Security as soon as possible. 2FA is mandatory for Enterprise plan administrators.

**Q: Can I customize my workspace's branding?**
Pro and Enterprise plans allow you to upload a custom logo and choose accent colors under Workspace Settings > Branding. These customizations apply across boards, shared links, and exported reports. Free and Starter plans use TaskFlow's default branding.

**Q: How long does it take to migrate an existing team to TaskFlow?**
Most teams complete migration within a day using our CSV or direct importers for tools like Trello and Asana. Larger organizations with complex workflows may take a few days to fully map custom fields and automations. Our support team offers free onboarding calls for Enterprise customers to speed up the process.

## Features & Usage

**Q: How do I create a recurring task?**
Open any task, click the "Repeat" icon, and choose a frequency such as daily, weekly, or custom intervals. A new instance of the task will automatically generate once the previous one is marked complete. Recurring tasks retain their original assignee and labels unless manually changed.

**Q: What is the difference between Board view and Timeline view?**
Board view displays tasks as cards organized into columns, ideal for visualizing workflow stages like a Kanban setup. Timeline view shows tasks along a calendar-based horizontal axis, better suited for tracking deadlines and dependencies. You can switch between views at any time without affecting the underlying task data.

**Q: How do I set up automations in TaskFlow?**
Go to Board Settings > Automations and choose a trigger, such as "task moved to Done," paired with an action like "notify assignee" or "archive task." Automations are available on Pro and Enterprise plans, with usage limits scaling by tier. You can test an automation rule before activating it.

**Q: Can I assign a task to multiple people?**
Yes, open the task and click the assignee field to add multiple team members as collaborators. Each assignee receives notifications for updates, but you can designate one person as the primary owner for accountability. Multiple assignees are supported on all plans.

**Q: How do custom fields work?**
Custom fields let you add structured data to tasks, such as priority levels, budget amounts, or client names, configurable under Board Settings > Custom Fields. They support text, number, dropdown, and date formats. Custom fields are available starting on the Pro plan.

**Q: How do I track time spent on a task?**
Enable the Time Tracking feature in Board Settings, then use the built-in timer on any task to log hours worked. You can also enter time manually if you forgot to start the timer. Time tracking reports are available in the Reporting dashboard for Pro and Enterprise plans.

**Q: Is there a way to see how much time my team has logged?**
Yes, the Reporting dashboard provides a breakdown of logged time by team member, board, or date range. This requires the Time Tracking feature to be enabled beforehand. Reports can be exported as CSV or PDF for sharing with stakeholders.

**Q: How do I create task dependencies?**
Open a task, select "Add Dependency," and choose whether it's blocked by or blocking another task. Dependent tasks are visually linked in Timeline view with connecting arrows. TaskFlow will warn you if completing a task out of dependency order may cause scheduling conflicts.

**Q: Can I create custom templates for recurring projects?**
Yes, save any board as a template by selecting "Save as Template" from the Board menu. Templates retain columns, custom fields, and automation rules, but exclude task assignees and due dates. Saved templates appear under "New Board > My Templates" for future use.

**Q: How do I filter tasks by assignee or due date?**
Use the Filter bar above any board to narrow tasks by assignee, due date, label, or custom field values. Filters can be combined and saved as a view for quick access later. Saved filtered views are personal by default but can be shared with the team.

## Troubleshooting

**Q: TaskFlow won't load in my browser — what should I do?**
First, try clearing your browser cache and cookies, then reload the page. If the issue persists, test in an incognito window or a different browser to rule out extension conflicts. TaskFlow officially supports the latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.

**Q: Why are my tasks not syncing between devices?**
Sync issues are usually caused by an unstable internet connection or an outdated app version. Check your connection, then update the TaskFlow app to the latest version from your app store or the Apps page. If the problem continues, try logging out and back in to force a full resync.

**Q: I'm not receiving email notifications. How do I fix this?**
Check your Notification Settings under Account Settings to confirm email alerts are enabled for the relevant boards. Also verify that emails from TaskFlow aren't being filtered into spam or blocked by your organization's email security policies. Whitelisting our domain usually resolves delivery issues.

**Q: Why does the desktop app keep crashing on startup?**
This is often caused by a corrupted local cache or an outdated app version. Try reinstalling the latest version from the Apps page, and if the crash continues, delete the app's local cache folder as described in our troubleshooting guide. Contact support with your crash logs if the issue persists.

**Q: My uploaded file attachment failed — what's wrong?**
File uploads can fail due to file size limits (250MB on Free/Starter, 5GB on Pro/Enterprise) or unstable network connections. Confirm your file is within the allowed size for your plan and try uploading again on a stable connection. Unsupported file types will also trigger an upload error message.

**Q: Why can't I see a board that a teammate shared with me?**
Check that you've been added as a member of the relevant workspace, since board access is governed by workspace membership and permissions. If you were only sent a direct link, you may need to request an invite from a Workspace Admin. Guest accounts only see boards they've been explicitly granted access to.

**Q: The automation I created isn't triggering. How do I troubleshoot it?**
Open Board Settings > Automations and check the rule's activity log to see if it's being triggered but failing silently, or not triggering at all. Common causes include conflicting automation rules or a trigger condition that no longer matches your board's current setup. Try disabling other automations temporarily to isolate the issue.

**Q: Why is TaskFlow running slowly on my account?**
Performance issues are often related to boards with a very large number of tasks (over 5,000) or too many active filters and automations running simultaneously. Archiving completed tasks and splitting large boards can significantly improve load times. Clearing your browser cache can also help if you're using the web app.

**Q: I accidentally deleted a task. Can I recover it?**
Deleted tasks are moved to the board's Trash for 30 days before permanent removal, and can be restored from Board Settings > Trash. After 30 days, deleted items cannot be recovered by TaskFlow support or any admin. We recommend archiving instead of deleting if you may need a task later.

**Q: Why am I getting logged out of TaskFlow repeatedly?**
Frequent logouts are typically caused by session timeout settings configured by your Workspace Admin, or by clearing cookies regularly in your browser. Enterprise plans with strict SSO session policies may also enforce shorter login durations. Check with your admin if this is affecting your whole team.

## Integrations

**Q: How do I connect TaskFlow to Slack?**
Go to Workspace Settings > Integrations > Slack and click "Connect," then authorize TaskFlow within your Slack workspace. Once connected, you can receive task notifications directly in Slack channels and create tasks using the `/taskflow` slash command. Admins can configure which boards send notifications to which channels.

**Q: Can I integrate TaskFlow with Google Calendar?**
Yes, under Workspace Settings > Integrations > Google Calendar, you can sync task due dates to a dedicated TaskFlow calendar. Changes made to due dates in TaskFlow automatically reflect in Google Calendar, though calendar-side edits do not sync back. This integration is available on all plans.

**Q: Does TaskFlow integrate with GitHub?**
Yes, the GitHub integration lets you link commits, branches, and pull requests directly to TaskFlow tasks. Once connected under Workspace Settings > Integrations > GitHub, mentioning a task ID in a commit message will automatically attach it to the corresponding task. This is especially useful for engineering teams tracking development progress.

**Q: How do I set up a Zapier connection with TaskFlow?**
Search for "TaskFlow" in Zapier's app directory and connect your account using an API key generated from Account Settings > API Access. From there, you can build Zaps that trigger TaskFlow actions based on events in thousands of other apps. Zapier integration is available starting on the Starter plan.

**Q: Can I use TaskFlow with Microsoft Teams?**
Yes, the Microsoft Teams integration mirrors the Slack integration, allowing task notifications and a `/taskflow` command directly within Teams channels. Set it up under Workspace Settings > Integrations > Microsoft Teams and authorize access through your Microsoft 365 admin if required. Notifications can be scoped per channel.

**Q: Is there a TaskFlow integration for Jira?**
Yes, the Jira integration allows two-way syncing of issues and tasks between the two platforms, useful for teams that split work between product and engineering tools. Configure field mapping under Workspace Settings > Integrations > Jira to control how statuses and priorities translate between systems. This integration is available on Pro and Enterprise plans.

**Q: How do I attach files from Google Drive to a task?**
Click the attachment icon on any task and select "Google Drive" to browse and link files without downloading them locally. You'll need to authorize Google Drive access the first time you use this feature under Workspace Settings > Integrations. Linked files always open the latest version stored in Drive.

**Q: Does TaskFlow support Dropbox file attachments?**
Yes, similar to Google Drive, you can attach Dropbox files directly to tasks via the attachment icon after authorizing the integration under Workspace Settings > Integrations > Dropbox. Files remain stored in Dropbox and TaskFlow only stores a reference link. This keeps your storage usage outside of TaskFlow's own file limits.

**Q: Can I build a custom integration using TaskFlow's API?**
Yes, TaskFlow offers a REST API with full documentation available under Account Settings > API Access, including endpoints for tasks, boards, and webhooks. API access is available on Pro and Enterprise plans, with rate limits scaling by tier. We also provide official SDKs for JavaScript and Python.

**Q: How do I disconnect an integration I no longer use?**
Navigate to Workspace Settings > Integrations, find the integration you want to remove, and click "Disconnect." This revokes TaskFlow's access permissions immediately, though any previously synced data, such as linked GitHub commits, will remain on existing tasks. You can reconnect the integration at any time without losing historical task data.

## Security & Privacy

**Q: How does TaskFlow protect my data?**
TaskFlow encrypts data in transit using TLS 1.2+ and at rest using AES-256 encryption. Our infrastructure is hosted on SOC 2-compliant cloud providers with regular third-party security audits. Access to production systems is restricted to authorized personnel under strict logging policies.

**Q: Is TaskFlow GDPR compliant?**
Yes, TaskFlow is fully GDPR compliant and provides tools for data export, rectification, and deletion in accordance with user rights under the regulation. Our Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is available for Enterprise customers upon request. EU customer data is stored in EU-based data centers when required.

**Q: How do I enable two-factor authentication on my account?**
Go to Account Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication and choose either an authenticator app or SMS-based verification. Once enabled, you'll need a verification code in addition to your password each time you log in from a new device. We strongly recommend using an authenticator app over SMS for stronger security.

**Q: Does TaskFlow support Single Sign-On (SSO)?**
Yes, SSO via SAML 2.0 is available on the Enterprise plan, supporting providers like Okta, Azure AD, and Google Workspace. Once configured under Workspace Settings > Security > SSO, all members can log in using your organization's identity provider. SSO can be enforced to disable password-based logins entirely.

**Q: Who can access my workspace's data?**
Access is governed by role-based access control (RBAC), meaning only Admins, Members, and Guests with explicit permissions can view workspace data. TaskFlow employees only access customer data with explicit consent for support purposes or as required by law. All internal access is logged and auditable.

**Q: How do I set permission levels for different team members?**
Under Workspace Settings > Members, you can assign each user a role of Admin, Member, or Guest, each with different levels of access to boards, billing, and settings. Admins have full control, Members can edit tasks and boards, and Guests have restricted view-or-comment access. Permissions can be adjusted at any time by a Workspace Admin.

**Q: What happens to my data if I delete my account?**
Account deletion permanently removes your personal data and workspace content after a 30-day grace period, during which you can cancel the deletion request. After this period, data is purged from active systems and removed from backups within 90 days. You can request a full data export before initiating deletion.

**Q: Can I export all my data before leaving TaskFlow?**
Yes, go to Workspace Settings > Data Export to download a complete archive of your boards, tasks, comments, and attachments in CSV and JSON formats. Large exports are processed asynchronously and emailed to you as a download link once ready. Exports are available regardless of your current plan.

**Q: Does TaskFlow undergo independent security audits?**
Yes, TaskFlow undergoes annual SOC 2 Type II audits performed by an independent third-party auditor, with reports available to Enterprise customers under NDA. We also run a continuous bug bounty program and periodic penetration testing. Summary audit results are published on our Trust Center page.

**Q: How long does TaskFlow retain deleted data before it's permanently erased?**
Deleted tasks and boards remain recoverable in Trash for 30 days, after which they are permanently purged from active databases. Backup copies are retained for an additional 60 days for disaster recovery purposes before being fully erased. This retention policy applies uniformly across all plans.
