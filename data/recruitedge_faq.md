# RecruitEdge Help Center — Frequently Asked Questions

## Account & Billing

**Q: How do I create a RecruitEdge account?**
Visit recruitedge.com/signup and enter your work email address to start a free 14-day trial. You'll be asked to set up your Workspace name and invite teammates once your email is verified. No credit card is required until you choose to upgrade to a paid plan.

**Q: What pricing plans does RecruitEdge offer?**
RecruitEdge offers three plans: Starter, Growth, and Enterprise. Starter is designed for small teams with basic Candidate Pipelines and Job Board Postings, Growth adds Interview Scheduling automation and Scorecards, and Enterprise includes SSO (SAML), Data Residency options, and a dedicated account manager. You can compare full feature breakdowns on the Pricing page inside your Workspace settings.

**Q: How do I upgrade from Starter to Growth?**
Go to Workspace Settings > Billing and click "Change Plan." Select Growth and confirm your payment details — the upgrade takes effect immediately, and you'll be billed a prorated amount for the remainder of your current cycle. Your existing Candidate Pipelines and data carry over automatically with no downtime.

**Q: I forgot my password — what should I do?**
Click "Forgot password?" on the login screen and enter the email associated with your account. We'll send a secure reset link valid for 30 minutes. If you don't receive the email within a few minutes, check your spam folder or contact support@recruitedge.com.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
You can reset your password anytime from Account Settings > Security by clicking "Change Password," or by using the "Forgot password?" link on the login page if you're locked out. For security, you'll be required to re-enter your current password before setting a new one if you're already logged in. Password resets via email link expire after 30 minutes.

**Q: Can I cancel my subscription at any time?**
Yes, you can cancel from Workspace Settings > Billing > Manage Subscription at any time, with no cancellation fees. Your account will remain active until the end of the current billing period, after which it converts to a read-only Starter account. You can reactivate a paid plan at any point to regain full access.

**Q: How is billing calculated for Enterprise accounts?**
Enterprise billing is based on the number of active Workspace Admin and recruiter seats, plus any add-ons like additional Data Residency regions. Invoices are generated monthly or annually depending on your contract terms, and your account manager can provide a custom quote. Annual Enterprise contracts receive a 15% discount compared to monthly billing.

**Q: Where can I find my invoices and billing history?**
All invoices are available under Workspace Settings > Billing > Invoice History, where you can download PDF copies for accounting purposes. Invoices are generated automatically at the start of each billing cycle and emailed to your designated billing contact. You can update the billing contact email at any time from the same page.

**Q: Does RecruitEdge offer discounts for nonprofits or startups?**
Yes, RecruitEdge offers a 20% discount on Growth and Enterprise plans for registered nonprofits and early-stage startups with fewer than 10 employees. Submit a request through the "Special Pricing" form on our website along with proof of eligibility. Approved discounts are applied automatically to your next billing cycle.

**Q: How do I add or remove users from my Workspace?**
Workspace Admins can manage users under Workspace Settings > Team Members by clicking "Invite Member" or selecting "Remove" next to an existing user. Removing a user immediately revokes their access but preserves their historical activity in the Audit Log. Seat-based plans like Growth and Enterprise will adjust your next invoice based on active user count.

## Setup & Installation

**Q: How do I set up my first Job Board Posting?**
From your dashboard, click "New Job Posting," fill in the role details, and choose which Candidate Pipeline stages to apply. You can then publish directly to your Careers Page Builder site or syndicate the listing to LinkedIn and Indeed with one click. Postings typically go live on external job boards within a few hours.

**Q: What are the system requirements to use RecruitEdge?**
RecruitEdge is a fully cloud-based platform accessible through any modern browser, including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge — no software installation is required. For the best experience, we recommend keeping your browser updated to the latest version. Mobile access is available through a responsive web app, with native iOS and Android apps currently in beta.

**Q: How do I install the RecruitEdge browser extension?**
Search for "RecruitEdge Sourcing Extension" in the Chrome Web Store and click "Add to Chrome." Once installed, sign in with your RecruitEdge credentials to start sourcing candidate profiles directly from LinkedIn into your Talent Pool. The extension currently supports Chrome and Edge browsers only.

**Q: How do I set up Single Sign-On (SSO) for my company?**
SSO (SAML) is available on the Enterprise plan and can be configured under Workspace Settings > Security > SSO Configuration. You'll need your identity provider's metadata file (Okta, Azure AD, or OneLogin are all supported) to complete setup. Once enabled, all Workspace members will be required to authenticate through your company's identity provider.

**Q: How do I create a new Candidate Pipeline for a role?**
Open the job posting and click "Pipeline Settings," then customize stages such as Applied, Phone Screen, Interview, Offer, and Hired to match your hiring process. You can save custom pipeline templates to reuse across future job postings. Drag-and-drop reordering lets you adjust stage order at any time.

**Q: I'm setting up RecruitEdge for the first time — where should I start?**
We recommend starting with the Setup Checklist that appears on your dashboard after signup, which walks you through creating your first Candidate Pipeline, inviting team members, and connecting at least one integration like Slack or Google Calendar. Most teams are fully onboarded within a day. Our onboarding specialists are also available via live chat during business hours.

**Q: How do I customize my Careers Page Builder site?**
Navigate to Workspace Settings > Careers Page and use the drag-and-drop editor to add your logo, brand colors, and company description. You can preview changes in real time before publishing, and the page automatically updates whenever you create new Job Board Postings. Custom domains are supported on Growth and Enterprise plans.

**Q: How do I import candidates from a spreadsheet?**
Go to Talent Pool > Import and upload a CSV file using our provided template, which maps columns like name, email, and resume link. RecruitEdge will validate the file and flag any formatting errors before import. Imported candidates are automatically tagged so you can filter them later.

**Q: Can I migrate my data from Greenhouse?**
Yes, RecruitEdge supports a guided Greenhouse import that transfers candidate records, job postings, and Scorecards into your new Workspace. Start the migration from Workspace Settings > Data Migration and follow the step-by-step wizard. Most migrations complete within 24 to 48 hours depending on data volume.

**Q: How do I set up Interview Scheduling for my team?**
Connect your Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook account under Integrations, then go to a candidate's profile and click "Schedule Interview" to view interviewer availability automatically. You can build reusable interview kits that bundle specific Scorecards with each interview stage. Calendar invites and Zoom links are generated automatically once a time is confirmed.

## Features & Usage

**Q: What is a Candidate Pipeline and how does it work?**
A Candidate Pipeline represents the stages a candidate moves through for a specific job, such as Applied, Phone Screen, Interview, Offer, and Hired. Recruiters can drag candidate cards between stages, leave notes, and trigger automated emails at each stage transition. Pipelines are fully customizable per job posting.

**Q: How do Scorecards work in RecruitEdge?**
Scorecards let interviewers rate candidates against predefined competencies and leave structured feedback after each interview. Hiring managers can review aggregated Scorecard results directly from the candidate's profile to make faster, more consistent decisions. Scorecard templates can be customized per role under Pipeline Settings.

**Q: How do I send an Offer Letter to a candidate?**
From the candidate's profile, click "Generate Offer Letter," select a template, and fill in compensation details. The letter can be sent for e-signature through our DocuSign integration, and you'll receive a notification once the candidate signs. Offer status is automatically reflected in the Candidate Pipeline.

**Q: How does Interview Scheduling handle multiple interviewers?**
Interview Scheduling checks the Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook availability of all selected interviewers and suggests overlapping time slots automatically. Once confirmed, calendar invites with Zoom links are sent to everyone involved, including the candidate. You can also set buffer times between interviews in your scheduling preferences.

**Q: What is the Talent Pool feature used for?**
The Talent Pool is a searchable database of all candidates who have applied or been sourced, even if they aren't currently in an active Candidate Pipeline. Recruiters can tag, filter, and re-engage past candidates for new roles without re-entering their information. Sourced LinkedIn profiles via our browser extension are also stored here.

**Q: How do I post a job to multiple job boards at once?**
When creating a Job Board Posting, select "Distribute to Job Boards" and check the boards you want to syndicate to, such as LinkedIn and Indeed. RecruitEdge automatically formats the listing to meet each board's requirements before publishing. You can track applicant source performance from the Job Posting analytics tab.

**Q: Can I customize the stages in my Candidate Pipeline?**
Yes, every Candidate Pipeline can be customized by adding, removing, or renaming stages under Pipeline Settings for that job posting. You can also set automated actions, like sending a rejection email when a candidate is moved to the "Not Selected" stage. Custom pipeline templates can be saved and reused for future postings.

**Q: How do I leave feedback on a candidate after an interview?**
Open the candidate's profile, select the relevant interview, and fill out the associated Scorecard with your ratings and comments. Feedback is visible to other team members involved in the hiring process to support collaborative decisions. You'll receive a reminder notification if a Scorecard is left incomplete 24 hours after the interview.

**Q: What reporting and analytics does RecruitEdge provide?**
RecruitEdge includes a Reports dashboard showing metrics like time-to-hire, pipeline conversion rates, and source performance across LinkedIn, Indeed, and your Careers Page Builder. Growth and Enterprise plans include customizable report exports in CSV format. Reports can be filtered by date range, job posting, or recruiter.

**Q: How do I move a candidate between stages in the pipeline?**
Simply drag and drop the candidate's card from one stage to the next within the Candidate Pipeline view, or use the "Move Stage" dropdown on their profile. Stage changes can trigger automated notifications to the candidate or hiring team, depending on your configured rules. All stage transitions are logged in the candidate's activity history.

## Troubleshooting

**Q: Why can't I log into my RecruitEdge account?**
First, confirm you're using the correct email address and that Caps Lock isn't affecting your password entry. If you've enabled Two-Factor Authentication (2FA), make sure your authenticator app's time is synced correctly, as codes expire quickly. If the issue persists, use the "Forgot password?" link or contact support@recruitedge.com for further assistance.

**Q: My Interview Scheduling isn't showing interviewer availability — what's wrong?**
This usually happens when the interviewer's Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook account hasn't been connected or the connection has expired. Ask the interviewer to reconnect their calendar under Account Settings > Integrations. If availability still doesn't appear after reconnecting, check that calendar sharing permissions allow RecruitEdge to read free/busy data.

**Q: Why are my Job Board Postings not appearing on LinkedIn or Indeed?**
New postings can take up to a few hours to appear on external job boards due to syndication processing times. If a posting still hasn't appeared after 24 hours, verify that your job description meets the minimum content requirements, as boards may reject incomplete listings. You can check syndication status under the Job Posting's "Distribution" tab.

**Q: The Scorecard I submitted isn't showing on the candidate's profile.**
This is typically caused by a temporary sync delay; try refreshing the candidate's profile page after a minute. If the Scorecard still doesn't appear, confirm you clicked "Submit" rather than "Save Draft," since drafts remain private until submitted. Contact support if the issue continues after these checks.

**Q: I'm not receiving email notifications from RecruitEdge.**
Check your notification preferences under Account Settings > Notifications to confirm the relevant alerts are enabled. Also check your spam or promotions folder, and add notifications@recruitedge.com to your safe senders list. If you use Slack notifications instead, verify the Slack integration is still connected under Workspace Settings > Integrations.

**Q: Why is the candidate import from my CSV file failing?**
Import failures usually occur when required columns like email or name are missing or mislabeled — download the latest CSV template from Talent Pool > Import to ensure correct formatting. Special characters or merged cells in spreadsheet software can also cause errors. The import wizard will display a row-by-row error log to help you correct the file.

**Q: Why does my DocuSign Offer Letter integration show an error?**
This error typically means your DocuSign authorization token has expired or been revoked. Reconnect DocuSign under Workspace Settings > Integrations by clicking "Reauthorize," then resend the Offer Letter. If the error persists, confirm your DocuSign account has sufficient envelope credits remaining.

**Q: The Careers Page Builder site isn't reflecting my recent changes.**
Published changes can take a few minutes to propagate due to caching on our content delivery network. Try clearing your browser cache or viewing the page in an incognito window. If changes still aren't visible after 15 minutes, click "Republish" from the Careers Page editor.

**Q: Why is Two-Factor Authentication not sending me a code?**
Confirm your phone number or authenticator app is correctly linked under Account Settings > Security, and check that your device has a stable network connection. SMS codes can occasionally be delayed by carrier networks, so authenticator apps are recommended for reliability. If you're permanently locked out, contact support to verify your identity and reset 2FA.

**Q: The system is running slowly when loading large Candidate Pipelines.**
Performance can be affected by pipelines containing several thousand candidates; we recommend using filters and archiving old, inactive candidates to improve load times. Clearing your browser cache and disabling unnecessary browser extensions can also help. Our engineering team continuously optimizes performance for high-volume Workspaces.

## Integrations

**Q: What integrations does RecruitEdge support?**
RecruitEdge integrates with LinkedIn and Indeed for Job Board Postings, Slack for hiring notifications, Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook for Interview Scheduling, Zoom for video interviews, and DocuSign for Offer Letters. Enterprise plans also support custom API integrations and Greenhouse data imports. New integrations are added regularly based on customer requests.

**Q: How do I connect Slack to RecruitEdge?**
Go to Workspace Settings > Integrations, click "Connect" next to Slack, and authorize the connection through your Slack Workspace admin. Once connected, you can choose which channels receive notifications for new applicants, Scorecard submissions, and Offer Letter signatures. You can disconnect Slack at any time from the same settings page.

**Q: Can I sync RecruitEdge with my Google Calendar?**
Yes, connect your Google Calendar under Account Settings > Integrations to enable automatic Interview Scheduling and two-way event syncing. Once connected, scheduled interviews appear on your calendar automatically, and changes made in either system stay in sync. Microsoft Outlook is supported as an alternative for teams that don't use Google Calendar.

**Q: How do I post jobs directly to LinkedIn?**
After connecting your LinkedIn account under Workspace Settings > Integrations, you can select LinkedIn as a distribution channel when creating a Job Board Posting. RecruitEdge automatically formats the listing to match LinkedIn's posting requirements. Note that LinkedIn may require a separate recruiter license for premium placement options.

**Q: Does RecruitEdge integrate with Zoom for video interviews?**
Yes, once you connect your Zoom account under Integrations, RecruitEdge automatically generates a Zoom meeting link whenever an interview is scheduled through Interview Scheduling. The link is included in calendar invites sent to both interviewers and candidates. Recording settings can be configured within your Zoom account preferences.

**Q: How do I set up the DocuSign integration for sending Offer Letters?**
Navigate to Workspace Settings > Integrations and click "Connect" next to DocuSign, then sign in and authorize access. Once connected, you can send any Offer Letter generated in RecruitEdge directly for e-signature. Signed documents are automatically attached to the candidate's profile.

**Q: Can I import data from Greenhouse into RecruitEdge?**
Yes, the Greenhouse import tool transfers candidates, job postings, and historical Scorecards into RecruitEdge using a guided migration wizard found under Workspace Settings > Data Migration. We recommend running the migration during off-peak hours to minimize disruption. Our support team can assist with large or complex migrations.

**Q: Does RecruitEdge have a public API?**
Yes, RecruitEdge offers a REST API available on Growth and Enterprise plans, allowing you to programmatically manage Candidate Pipelines, job postings, and Talent Pool records. API keys can be generated under Workspace Settings > API Access. Full documentation is available at developers.recruitedge.com.

**Q: How do I disconnect an integration I no longer use?**
Go to Workspace Settings > Integrations, find the integration you want to remove, and click "Disconnect." This immediately revokes RecruitEdge's access to that third-party account, though historical data already synced will remain in your Workspace. You can reconnect the integration at any time if needed.

**Q: Why isn't my Indeed job posting syncing properly?**
Indeed sync issues are often caused by listings that don't meet their content guidelines, such as missing salary information in certain regions. Check the "Distribution" tab on the job posting for a specific rejection reason from Indeed. If the listing appears compliant but still fails, contact support for manual review.

## Security & Privacy

**Q: How does RecruitEdge protect candidate data?**
RecruitEdge encrypts all data in transit using TLS 1.2+ and at rest using AES-256 encryption. Access to candidate information is restricted by role-based permissions, and all access events are recorded in the Audit Log. We undergo regular third-party security audits to maintain compliance with industry standards.

**Q: Is RecruitEdge GDPR compliant?**
Yes, RecruitEdge is fully GDPR compliant and provides tools for candidate data subject requests, including data export and the "right to be forgotten" deletion requests. Workspace Admins can manage these requests under Workspace Settings > Privacy. We also offer Data Residency options for EU-based customers on Enterprise plans.

**Q: What is Two-Factor Authentication and how do I enable it?**
Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) adds an extra layer of security by requiring a one-time code from an authenticator app or SMS in addition to your password at login. You can enable it under Account Settings > Security by scanning a QR code with an authenticator app like Google Authenticator or Authy. We strongly recommend enabling 2FA for all Workspace Admin accounts.

**Q: Where is my Workspace data stored?**
By default, Workspace data is stored in our primary data centers located in the United States. Enterprise customers can request Data Residency in the EU or other supported regions to meet local compliance requirements. Contact your account manager to configure regional data storage for your Workspace.

**Q: How do I view the Audit Log for my Workspace?**
Workspace Admins can access the Audit Log under Workspace Settings > Security > Audit Log, which records actions like login attempts, candidate data exports, and permission changes. Logs can be filtered by user, date range, or action type, and exported for compliance reviews. Audit Log retention is 12 months on Growth and unlimited on Enterprise.

**Q: Can I control which team members have access to specific Candidate Pipelines?**
Yes, Workspace Admins can configure role-based permissions under Workspace Settings > Team Members to restrict access to specific Candidate Pipelines, job postings, or candidate data. This is especially useful for confidential searches or executive-level hiring. Permission changes are logged automatically in the Audit Log.

**Q: Does RecruitEdge sell or share candidate data with third parties?**
No, RecruitEdge does not sell candidate data to third parties under any circumstances. Data is only shared with integrations you explicitly connect, such as LinkedIn or DocuSign, and solely for the purpose of completing the action you initiated. Our full data handling practices are detailed in our Privacy Policy.

**Q: How long does RecruitEdge retain candidate data?**
By default, candidate data is retained for 24 months after the last activity on their profile, after which it is automatically anonymized unless your Workspace has configured a custom retention policy. Workspace Admins can adjust retention settings under Workspace Settings > Privacy. Candidates can also request earlier deletion in compliance with applicable privacy laws.

**Q: How do I enable Single Sign-On for added account security?**
SSO (SAML) is available on the Enterprise plan and can be configured by a Workspace Admin under Workspace Settings > Security > SSO Configuration using your identity provider's metadata. Once enabled, you can also enforce SSO-only login to prevent password-based access entirely. This significantly reduces the risk of credential-based attacks.

**Q: What should I do if I suspect unauthorized access to my account?**
Immediately change your password and enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) if it isn't already active, then review your Audit Log for any suspicious login activity. Contact support@recruitedge.com right away so our security team can investigate and, if needed, revoke active sessions. We recommend all Workspace Admins review connected integrations periodically as an added precaution.
