# TimeTrackr Help Center — Frequently Asked Questions

## Account & Billing

**Q: How do I create a TimeTrackr account?**
You can sign up at timetrackr.com/signup using your work email address. After verifying your email, you'll be guided through a short setup wizard to create your company workspace and choose a pricing plan. No credit card is required during the 14-day free trial.

**Q: What pricing plans does TimeTrackr offer?**
TimeTrackr offers three plans: Starter (best for teams under 10 employees), Growth (our most popular plan, with overtime rules and geofencing included), and Enterprise (custom pricing with dedicated support and SSO). You can compare plan features in the Billing section of your account settings.

**Q: How do I upgrade or downgrade my subscription plan?**
Go to Settings > Billing > Manage Plan and select the new plan you'd like to switch to. Upgrades take effect immediately, while downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. Any prorated charges or credits will appear on your next invoice.

**Q: I forgot my password. What should I do?**
Click "Forgot Password" on the login screen and enter the email address associated with your TimeTrackr account. You'll receive a password reset link valid for 30 minutes. If you don't see the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
Navigate to the login page and select "Forgot Password," then follow the emailed link to set a new password. For security, your new password must be at least 10 characters and include a number and a symbol. You'll be logged out of all devices once the reset is complete.

**Q: Can I change the email address on my account?**
Yes. Go to Settings > Profile > Account Email and enter your new email address. You'll need to confirm the change via a verification link sent to the new address before it takes effect.

**Q: How is billing calculated for the Growth plan?**
The Growth plan is billed per active employee per month, with active employees defined as anyone who clocks in at least once during the billing period. You can view a detailed breakdown of charges under Billing > Invoices. Annual billing is also available at a 15% discount.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
TimeTrackr accepts all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) as well as ACH bank transfers for Enterprise plan customers. Payment information can be updated anytime under Settings > Billing > Payment Methods.

**Q: How do I cancel my TimeTrackr subscription?**
Go to Settings > Billing > Manage Plan and click "Cancel Subscription" at the bottom of the page. Your account will remain active until the end of the current billing period, and you can export your timesheets and payroll data before the cancellation date.

**Q: Do you offer refunds if I cancel mid-cycle?**
TimeTrackr does not provide prorated refunds for mid-cycle cancellations on monthly plans. Annual plan cancellations within the first 30 days are eligible for a full refund; after that, refunds are issued on a case-by-case basis. Contact billing@timetrackr.com for assistance.

## Setup & Installation

**Q: How do I install the TimeTrackr desktop app?**
Download the installer for Windows or macOS from timetrackr.com/download and run the setup file. Once installed, log in with your TimeTrackr credentials to sync your company workspace. The desktop app supports automatic idle detection and offline time tracking.

**Q: How do I set up Kiosk Mode for a shared device?**
Go to Settings > Time Clocks > Kiosk Mode and select "Enable Kiosk Mode" on the device you want to designate as a shared clock-in station. Employees can then clock in and out using a 4-digit PIN or photo verification on that device. Kiosk Mode is available on Growth and Enterprise plans.

**Q: What's the process for setting up a new employee?**
From the Team tab, click "Add Employee" and fill in their name, email, pay rate, and assigned overtime rule. The new employee will receive an invitation email with instructions to set up their TimeTrackr login. You can also bulk-import employees using a CSV template.

**Q: How do I add a new team member to TimeTrackr?**
Click "Add Employee" under the Team tab and complete their profile, including department and manager assignment. An invite link is automatically emailed to them, valid for 7 days. Once accepted, they'll appear in your active employee count for billing purposes.

**Q: How do I install TimeTrackr on a mobile device?**
Search for "TimeTrackr" on the Apple App Store or Google Play Store and install the app. Sign in with your existing TimeTrackr credentials, and enable location permissions if your company uses geofencing. Mobile clock-ins sync automatically with your web dashboard.

**Q: How do I set up geofencing for job sites?**
Go to Settings > Locations > Geofencing and add the address or GPS coordinates of each job site, along with a radius (typically 100–500 feet). Employees assigned to that location will only be able to clock in when within the defined geofence. Geofencing requires the Growth plan or higher.

**Q: Can I configure custom overtime rules?**
Yes. Under Settings > Overtime Rules, you can create custom rules based on daily thresholds, weekly thresholds, or state-specific labor laws (such as California's daily overtime rule). Each employee or department can be assigned a different overtime rule as needed.

**Q: How do I set up overtime rules for employees in different states?**
In Settings > Overtime Rules, create a separate rule profile for each state and assign employees to the correct profile based on their work location. TimeTrackr includes pre-built templates for federal FLSA rules and several state-specific variations. You can edit thresholds at any time, and changes apply going forward only.

**Q: How do I connect my time clock hardware to TimeTrackr?**
TimeTrackr supports compatible biometric time clocks via the Settings > Time Clocks > Hardware Integration page. Enter the device's serial number and pairing code to link it to your workspace. Once paired, clock-in data syncs to your timesheets in real time.

**Q: How do I set up departments and locations?**
Go to Settings > Organization Structure to create departments, job sites, and reporting hierarchies. You can then assign employees to specific departments and locations, which feeds into timesheet filtering and geofencing rules. This structure also determines how labor cost reports are grouped.

## Features & Usage

**Q: How do employees clock in and out?**
Employees can clock in and out through the TimeTrackr mobile app, desktop app, web dashboard, or a Kiosk Mode device, depending on your company's setup. Each clock-in is timestamped and, if geofencing is enabled, validated against the employee's assigned location. Managers can view live clock status from the Team Activity dashboard.

**Q: How do I approve timesheets?**
Go to the Timesheets tab, select the pay period, and review each employee's logged hours. Click "Approve" individually or use "Approve All" to process the entire batch at once. Approved timesheets are locked from further edits unless a manager manually reopens them.

**Q: Can employees edit their own timesheets?**
Employees can request edits to their timesheets, but changes require manager approval before they're finalized. This is controlled under Settings > Permissions > Timesheet Editing, which can be toggled per role. All edit requests are logged with a timestamp and reason for auditing purposes.

**Q: How do I generate a payroll report?**
Navigate to Reports > Payroll Summary, select your date range and employee group, then click "Generate Report." The report includes regular hours, overtime, and any approved time-off, formatted for direct export to your payroll provider. You can also schedule recurring reports to be emailed automatically.

**Q: What's the difference between a timesheet and a time clock entry?**
A time clock entry is the raw clock-in/clock-out record generated when an employee uses the time clock. A timesheet aggregates these entries over a pay period into a reviewable, approvable summary, including totals for regular and overtime hours. Managers work primarily with timesheets, while time clock entries serve as the underlying audit trail.

**Q: How do I track paid time off (PTO) in TimeTrackr?**
Go to the Time Off tab to set up PTO policies, including accrual rates and carryover limits. Employees can submit time-off requests directly through the app, which managers approve or deny from the same dashboard. Approved PTO automatically appears on the relevant timesheet.

**Q: Can I set up automatic break deductions?**
Yes. Under Settings > Break Rules, you can configure automatic unpaid break deductions (for example, 30 minutes after 6 hours worked). These deductions apply automatically to timesheets and can be overridden manually if an employee did not take their break. Break rules can vary by location to comply with local labor laws.

**Q: How do I run a labor cost report by department?**
Go to Reports > Labor Costs, then filter by department or location using the dropdown menus. The report breaks down total wages, overtime costs, and PTO costs for the selected group and date range. Results can be exported as a CSV or PDF for further analysis.

**Q: Is there a way to see who is currently clocked in?**
Yes, the Team Activity dashboard on the main screen shows a live list of all employees currently clocked in, along with their location if geofencing is enabled. Managers can also see who is on a break or has forgotten to clock out. This view updates in real time.

**Q: How do I correct a missed clock-out?**
Go to the employee's timesheet, locate the incomplete entry, and click "Edit Entry" to manually add the correct clock-out time. A reason for the edit is required and will be saved in the audit log. Employees can also submit a correction request from their mobile app for manager approval.

## Troubleshooting

**Q: The mobile app won't let me clock in. What should I do?**
First, confirm that location services are enabled for TimeTrackr if your company uses geofencing, since clock-ins outside the defined radius will be blocked. Next, check that you have a stable internet connection, as the app needs to sync with the server. If the issue persists, try logging out and back in, or contact your administrator to confirm your employee profile is active.

**Q: Why can't I clock in from the mobile app?**
This usually happens when geofencing is enabled and you're outside the approved job site radius, or when the app has lost connectivity. Open Settings on your phone and verify TimeTrackr has location permission set to "Always" or "While Using the App." If the problem continues after restarting the app, reach out to your manager to confirm your location assignment is correct.

**Q: My timesheet hours look incorrect. How do I fix this?**
Check whether automatic break deductions or overtime rules are being applied differently than expected, as these can affect total hours shown. Review individual time clock entries for the pay period to spot any missed or duplicate punches. If you still see a discrepancy, submit a correction request or contact your manager to review the entry manually.

**Q: TimeTrackr is showing duplicate clock-in entries. What's going on?**
Duplicate entries typically occur when an employee clocks in from both the mobile app and a Kiosk Mode device within a short window, or when there's a brief loss of connectivity that causes a retry. Go to the employee's timesheet, identify the duplicate entry, and delete the incorrect one with a noted reason. We recommend assigning each employee a single primary clock-in method to avoid this.

**Q: I'm not receiving email notifications from TimeTrackr.**
First, check your spam or junk folder, and confirm that notifications@timetrackr.com is added to your safe senders list. Then verify your notification preferences under Settings > Notifications haven't been turned off for that alert type. If emails still aren't arriving, your IT department may need to whitelist our sending domain.

**Q: The desktop app is stuck on "Syncing." What should I do?**
This is usually caused by a temporary loss of internet connection or a large backlog of offline time entries waiting to sync. Try closing and reopening the app, and ensure your computer's clock and time zone settings are accurate. If syncing remains stuck after a few minutes, restart your computer and check for app updates.

**Q: Why is overtime not calculating correctly on a timesheet?**
Verify that the employee is assigned to the correct overtime rule under their profile in Settings > Team > Employee Details, since mismatched state or department rules are the most common cause. Also confirm the pay period start and end dates align with your overtime rule's weekly threshold. If the rule looks correct but hours still seem off, contact support with the employee's timesheet ID for investigation.

**Q: Geofencing is blocking employees who are at the correct job site.**
This typically happens when the geofence radius is set too narrow or the job site's GPS coordinates were entered slightly off. Go to Settings > Locations > Geofencing and widen the radius or re-pin the exact coordinates using the map tool. Keep in mind that GPS accuracy can vary by device, so a radius of at least 150 feet is recommended for most sites.

**Q: I can't log into my TimeTrackr account even with the correct password.**
Make sure Caps Lock is off and that you're using the email address associated with your account, not a personal alias. If your company uses single sign-on (SSO), you may need to log in through your organization's identity provider instead of the standard login page. If the issue continues, use the "Forgot Password" link to reset your credentials.

**Q: My QuickBooks export is showing a sync error.**
Sync errors are most often caused by an expired authentication token or a mismatch between employee names in TimeTrackr and QuickBooks. Go to Settings > Integrations > QuickBooks and click "Reconnect" to refresh the authentication token. If the error persists, confirm that each employee's name matches exactly between both systems.

## Integrations

**Q: Does TimeTrackr integrate with QuickBooks?**
Yes, TimeTrackr offers a native QuickBooks Online integration that syncs approved timesheets directly into payroll runs. You can map TimeTrackr employees to their QuickBooks counterparts under Settings > Integrations > QuickBooks. Syncing can be set to automatic after each pay period or run manually.

**Q: How do I connect TimeTrackr to Gusto for payroll?**
Go to Settings > Integrations > Gusto and click "Connect," then log in with your Gusto administrator credentials to authorize the integration. Once connected, approved timesheets automatically flow into your next Gusto payroll run. Any pay rate changes made in Gusto will also sync back to TimeTrackr.

**Q: Can I integrate TimeTrackr with Slack?**
Yes. Under Settings > Integrations > Slack, click "Add to Slack" and authorize the connection to your workspace. Once enabled, employees can clock in and out directly from Slack using the /timetrackr command, and managers can receive daily summary notifications in a designated channel.

**Q: Is there a Google Calendar integration?**
Yes, TimeTrackr's Google Calendar integration syncs approved PTO and scheduled shifts directly to an employee's calendar. Connect it under Settings > Integrations > Google Calendar by signing in with your Google account. Calendar events update automatically whenever a shift or time-off request changes.

**Q: How do I set up the QuickBooks integration step by step?**
Navigate to Settings > Integrations > QuickBooks and click "Connect Account," then sign in with your QuickBooks Online credentials when prompted. Next, map each TimeTrackr employee to their corresponding QuickBooks employee record to ensure accurate payroll exports. Once mapping is complete, you can run a test sync before enabling automatic exports.

**Q: Does TimeTrackr support payroll providers other than Gusto and QuickBooks?**
Yes, TimeTrackr also integrates with ADP and Paychex on the Growth and Enterprise plans, in addition to Gusto and QuickBooks. Each integration is configured under Settings > Integrations and uses a similar authentication and employee-mapping process. If your payroll provider isn't listed, you can always export timesheets as a CSV for manual import.

**Q: Can I sync employee schedules with Google Calendar?**
Yes, once the Google Calendar integration is enabled under Settings > Integrations, scheduled shifts created in TimeTrackr's Scheduling tool automatically appear on each employee's connected calendar. Updates to a shift, such as a time change, will sync within a few minutes. Employees can disconnect their personal calendar sync at any time from their profile settings.

**Q: How do I disconnect an integration like Slack or QuickBooks?**
Go to Settings > Integrations, select the integration you want to remove, and click "Disconnect." This revokes TimeTrackr's access token but does not delete any historical data that was already synced. You can reconnect the integration at any time by re-authorizing access.

**Q: Does TimeTrackr offer an API for custom integrations?**
Yes, Enterprise plan customers have access to the TimeTrackr REST API, which supports endpoints for employees, timesheets, time clock entries, and payroll exports. API documentation and key generation are available under Settings > Integrations > Developer API. Rate limits and authentication details are included in the developer portal.

**Q: Why isn't my Gusto sync pulling in updated pay rates?**
Pay rate updates only sync from Gusto to TimeTrackr during the nightly sync window, so changes made during the day may take up to 24 hours to appear. You can also trigger a manual sync under Settings > Integrations > Gusto by clicking "Sync Now." If rates still don't update after a manual sync, verify the employee mapping hasn't been altered.

## Security & Privacy

**Q: How does TimeTrackr protect my company's data?**
TimeTrackr encrypts all data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and encrypts stored data at rest using AES-256. Our infrastructure is hosted on SOC 2 Type II-compliant cloud servers with regular third-party security audits. Access to production systems is restricted to authorized personnel only.

**Q: Is my employee data encrypted?**
Yes, all employee data, including time clock entries, pay rates, and personal information, is encrypted both in transit and at rest. We also enforce role-based access controls so only authorized managers and admins can view sensitive payroll information. Encryption keys are rotated regularly as part of our security protocol.

**Q: Does TimeTrackr support single sign-on (SSO)?**
Yes, SSO is available on the Enterprise plan and supports SAML 2.0 providers such as Okta, Azure AD, and Google Workspace. Once configured under Settings > Security > SSO, employees can log in using their existing corporate credentials. SSO also allows for centralized user provisioning and deprovisioning.

**Q: How do I enable two-factor authentication (2FA)?**
Go to Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication and click "Enable 2FA," then scan the QR code with an authenticator app like Google Authenticator or Authy. You'll be asked to enter a verification code at each new login going forward. We strongly recommend enabling 2FA for all admin and manager accounts.

**Q: Who can see my GPS location data from geofencing?**
GPS location data collected for geofencing is only visible to managers and admins assigned to oversee that employee's location or department. TimeTrackr does not track employee location outside of clock-in and clock-out events. Location data retention follows the same policy as other timesheet data and can be configured by your administrator.

**Q: What is TimeTrackr's data retention policy?**
By default, TimeTrackr retains timesheet, time clock, and payroll export data for 7 years to support compliance with labor recordkeeping laws. Administrators on the Enterprise plan can request custom retention periods to align with specific regional regulations. Data scheduled for deletion is permanently purged within 30 days of the retention period ending.

**Q: How do I delete my TimeTrackr account and personal data?**
Account deletion requests can be submitted by your company administrator through Settings > Security > Data Requests, or by emailing privacy@timetrackr.com. Once verified, personal data is removed from active systems within 30 days, except where retention is legally required for payroll records. You'll receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.

**Q: Is TimeTrackr compliant with GDPR?**
Yes, TimeTrackr is GDPR-compliant and offers data processing agreements (DPAs) for customers operating in the European Union. We provide tools for data export, correction, and deletion requests in line with GDPR's data subject rights. Our privacy policy and DPA template are available under Settings > Security > Compliance.

**Q: Can employees see each other's pay rates or timesheets?**
No, by default employees can only view their own timesheets and pay information. Visibility into other employees' data is restricted by role-based permissions, which administrators configure under Settings > Permissions. Only managers and admins with the appropriate role can view team-wide payroll details.

**Q: What happens to our data if we cancel our TimeTrackr subscription?**
After cancellation, your data remains accessible in a read-only state for 90 days, allowing you to export timesheets, payroll reports, and employee records. After this period, data is archived according to our standard retention policy and eventually purged unless otherwise required by law. We recommend exporting all needed reports before your cancellation date.
