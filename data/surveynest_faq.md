# SurveyNest Help Center — Frequently Asked Questions

## Account & Billing

**Q: How do I create a SurveyNest account?**
You can sign up at app.surveynest.com/signup using your email address or by connecting a Google or Microsoft account. After verifying your email, you'll be guided through a short onboarding flow to set up your first survey. No credit card is required to start on the free Starter plan.

**Q: What pricing plans does SurveyNest offer?**
SurveyNest offers four plans based on monthly response volume: Starter (free, up to 100 responses/month), Growth ($29/month, up to 2,500 responses/month), Pro ($79/month, up to 15,000 responses/month), and Enterprise (custom pricing, unlimited responses). All paid plans include a 14-day free trial.

**Q: How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?**
Go to Account Settings > Billing and select "Change Plan." You can move between Starter, Growth, Pro, and Enterprise at any time, and changes take effect immediately with a prorated charge or credit. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle to avoid losing access to features you're actively using.

**Q: I forgot my password. What should I do?**
Click "Forgot password?" on the login screen and enter the email address associated with your account. We'll send a secure reset link that expires after 30 minutes. If you don't receive the email within a few minutes, check your spam folder or contact support.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
Navigate to the login page and select "Forgot password?" to receive a password reset link via email. Once you click the link, you'll be prompted to create a new password that meets our minimum security requirements. For security reasons, you'll be logged out of all active sessions after the reset.

**Q: Can I cancel my subscription at any time?**
Yes, you can cancel anytime from Account Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription. Your account will remain active on your current paid plan until the end of the billing period, after which it automatically reverts to the free Starter plan. No cancellation fees apply.

**Q: Do you offer refunds?**
We offer full refunds within 14 days of an initial paid subscription purchase if you're not satisfied. Refund requests after that window are evaluated on a case-by-case basis by our billing team. To request a refund, email billing@surveynest.com with your account email and reason for cancellation.

**Q: How do I update my billing information or payment method?**
Go to Account Settings > Billing > Payment Methods to add, remove, or update a credit card on file. SurveyNest uses Stripe to process all payments, so your card details are never stored on our servers. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

**Q: Can I add team members to my account?**
Yes, Growth, Pro, and Enterprise plans support multiple team members with role-based permissions (Admin, Editor, Viewer). Go to Account Settings > Team to invite collaborators by email. Starter plan accounts are limited to a single user.

**Q: Where can I find my invoices and billing history?**
All past invoices are available under Account Settings > Billing > Invoice History, where you can view or download them as PDFs. Invoices are also automatically emailed to your billing contact each cycle. If you need a corrected invoice for accounting purposes, contact billing@surveynest.com.

## Setup & Installation

**Q: How do I create my first survey?**
After logging in, click "New Survey" from your dashboard and choose to start from a blank survey or one of our pre-built templates. You can then add questions, customize the theme, and configure question logic before publishing. Most users have a basic survey live within ten minutes.

**Q: How do I install the SurveyNest embeddable widget on my website?**
Open your survey, go to the "Share" tab, and select "Embed." Copy the provided JavaScript snippet and paste it into your site's HTML where you want the widget to appear, or use the inline, popup, or slide-in embed options. The embeddable widget automatically inherits your selected theme and updates live as you make changes.

**Q: What's the easiest way to get a survey up and running quickly?**
The fastest path is to pick a template from our gallery, adjust the wording to match your use case, and hit Publish. Templates already include sensible question logic and a default theme, so you can skip most of the setup work. You can always customize further after publishing.

**Q: How do I set up a custom domain for my survey links?**
Custom domains are available on Pro and Enterprise plans under Account Settings > Domains. Add your domain, then create the CNAME record we provide with your DNS provider. Propagation typically takes a few hours, after which your survey links will use your branded URL.

**Q: Do I need to install any software to use SurveyNest?**
No, SurveyNest is entirely web-based and works through any modern browser at app.surveynest.com. There's nothing to download or install, and surveys you build can be distributed via shareable links or the embeddable widget without any plugins. We also offer optional native apps for iOS and Android for managing surveys on the go.

**Q: How do I add my company logo and brand colors?**
Open the Themes panel within your survey editor and select "Customize Theme." From there you can upload your logo, set brand colors, and choose fonts that will apply across all your surveys using that theme. Themes can be saved and reused across multiple surveys for consistent branding.

**Q: How do I duplicate or clone an existing survey?**
From your dashboard, click the three-dot menu next to any survey and select "Duplicate." This creates an exact copy, including all questions, logic, and theme settings, which you can then edit independently. This is useful for running seasonal variations of a recurring survey.

**Q: How do I set up question logic and branching for my survey?**
Open the question you want to add logic to, click "Add Logic," and define rules such as "If answer equals X, skip to question Y." Question logic and branching lets you create personalized paths so respondents only see questions relevant to their previous answers. Logic rules can be layered to support complex multi-path surveys.

**Q: Can I import an existing survey from another tool?**
Yes, SurveyNest supports importing surveys via CSV question lists or directly from Google Forms using our import wizard under "New Survey" > "Import." Question types are automatically mapped to the closest SurveyNest equivalent. You may need to manually reconfigure advanced logic after import.

**Q: How do I publish a survey so people can respond to it?**
Once your survey is ready, click "Publish" in the top right corner of the editor. You'll receive a shareable link immediately, and you can also generate an embeddable widget or QR code from the same Share tab. Published surveys can be unpublished or edited at any time without losing collected responses.

## Features & Usage

**Q: What question types does SurveyNest support?**
SurveyNest supports multiple choice, checkboxes, short text, long text, rating scales, Net Promoter Score, date pickers, file uploads, and matrix questions. Each question type can be customized with validation rules and required-field settings. New question types are added periodically based on customer requests.

**Q: How does question logic and branching work?**
Question logic and branching lets you show or skip questions based on a respondent's previous answers, creating a personalized survey path. You configure rules directly on each question, such as "If respondent selects 'No,' skip to Question 8." This keeps surveys shorter and more relevant for each individual respondent.

**Q: How do I view response analytics for my survey?**
Open your survey and click the "Analytics" tab to see real-time response analytics, including completion rates, average time to complete, and per-question breakdowns. You can filter analytics by date range, respondent segment, or specific question. Analytics data can also be exported as CSV or PDF.

**Q: Can I customize the look and feel of my survey with themes?**
Yes, SurveyNest includes a Themes panel where you can choose from pre-built themes or build a custom one with your own colors, fonts, and logo. Themes apply consistently across the survey, the embeddable widget, and confirmation pages. You can save multiple themes and switch between them per survey.

**Q: What's the difference between response analytics and raw response data?**
Response analytics provides aggregated insights like charts, completion rates, and trend lines, while raw response data gives you the individual, unprocessed answers from each respondent. Both are accessible from the Analytics tab, with raw data available under the "Responses" sub-tab. You can export either view to CSV for further analysis.

**Q: How do I add collaborators to edit a survey with me?**
Open the survey, click "Share," and select "Invite Collaborator." Enter their email and choose an Editor or Viewer role; this feature is available on Growth, Pro, and Enterprise plans. Collaborators will see real-time changes as you build the survey together.

**Q: Can I set a response limit or closing date for my survey?**
Yes, under the survey's "Settings" tab you can set a maximum number of responses or a specific closing date and time. Once either limit is reached, the survey automatically closes and displays a custom message you define. This is useful for capped giveaways or time-sensitive feedback windows.

**Q: How do I create a recurring or scheduled survey, like a monthly NPS check-in?**
Use the "Schedule" option under survey Settings to set a recurring send cadence, such as monthly or quarterly, when paired with email distribution. Combine this with the Net Promoter Score question type and response analytics to track sentiment trends over time. Scheduled surveys can be paused or edited without disrupting future sends.

**Q: How do I add my own custom CSS to a survey?**
Pro and Enterprise plans allow custom CSS injection under Themes > Advanced > Custom CSS. This lets you fine-tune styling beyond what the standard theme editor supports, such as custom button shapes or spacing. Be cautious with custom CSS, as it can override default responsive behavior on mobile devices.

**Q: Does SurveyNest support multilingual surveys?**
Yes, you can add multiple language versions of a survey under Settings > Languages, and respondents will see the version matching their browser's language setting. Question logic and branching rules apply consistently across all language versions. Translations can be entered manually or imported via CSV.

## Troubleshooting

**Q: My survey responses aren't showing up in analytics. What should I do?**
First, confirm the survey is published and not in draft mode, since draft responses aren't recorded. Next, check the Analytics tab's date range filter, as it may be excluding recent responses. If responses still don't appear after a few minutes, contact support@surveynest.com with your survey ID.

**Q: The embeddable widget isn't loading on my website.**
Make sure the embed snippet was pasted correctly and hasn't been altered, and check your browser's console for any script-blocking errors from ad blockers or content security policies. Confirm your survey is published, since embeds for draft surveys won't render. If the issue persists, try regenerating the embed code from the Share tab.

**Q: I can't log in to my account. What should I check?**
Double-check that you're using the correct email address and that Caps Lock isn't affecting your password entry. If you signed up using Google or Microsoft sign-in, make sure you're using that same option rather than a password. If problems continue, use the "Forgot password?" link to reset your credentials.

**Q: Why am I not receiving email notifications for new responses?**
Check Account Settings > Notifications to confirm response alerts are enabled for the specific survey. Also check your spam or promotions folder, as automated emails are sometimes filtered there. If notifications are enabled and still not arriving, verify your account email address is correct and up to date.

**Q: Question logic and branching isn't skipping questions the way I expect.**
Review your logic rules in order, since SurveyNest evaluates them top to bottom and the first matching rule takes priority. A common issue is conflicting rules on the same question that create unreachable paths. Use the "Preview" mode to test each branch path before publishing changes.

**Q: My survey theme looks different on mobile than on desktop.**
Some custom CSS or font choices may not scale well on smaller screens, so we recommend testing your theme using the built-in mobile preview before publishing. Matrix questions and wide tables are especially prone to mobile display issues. If the problem persists, try reverting to a default theme to isolate whether custom styling is the cause.

**Q: I'm getting an error when trying to export response data.**
Large exports, typically those with tens of thousands of responses, can occasionally time out; try narrowing the date range or filtering by specific questions before exporting. Ensure your browser allows file downloads from app.surveynest.com. If the error includes a specific error code, include it when contacting support.

**Q: My integration with Zapier or Slack stopped working.**
Check the Integrations tab to confirm the connection still shows as "Active," since third-party tokens can expire or be revoked. Reauthorize the integration by disconnecting and reconnecting your Zapier or Slack account. Also verify the linked Zap or Slack channel hasn't been deleted on the third-party side.

**Q: Why can't respondents submit the survey?**
This is often caused by a required question being left blank or a validation rule, such as an email format check, not being met. Confirm the survey hasn't hit its response limit or closing date under Settings. If the "Submit" button appears unresponsive, ask the respondent to try a different browser or clear their cache.

**Q: I accidentally deleted a survey. Can I recover it?**
Deleted surveys are moved to the Trash folder on your dashboard and remain recoverable for 30 days before permanent deletion. Go to Trash, select the survey, and click "Restore" to bring it back along with its collected responses. After 30 days, deleted surveys cannot be recovered by our support team.

## Integrations

**Q: What integrations does SurveyNest support?**
SurveyNest integrates natively with Mailchimp, Google Sheets, Slack, and Zapier, with additional integrations available on Pro and Enterprise plans. These let you automatically sync responses, trigger notifications, or connect to hundreds of other apps through Zapier. New native integrations are added based on customer demand.

**Q: How do I connect SurveyNest to Google Sheets?**
Go to the Integrations tab within your survey, select Google Sheets, and authorize access to your Google account. Choose an existing spreadsheet or let SurveyNest create a new one, and each new response will automatically populate as a new row. You can map specific questions to specific columns during setup.

**Q: How do I send survey responses to a Slack channel?**
Open the Integrations tab, select Slack, and authorize SurveyNest to post to your workspace. Choose the channel where you'd like new response notifications to appear, and optionally customize which question answers are included in the message. This is a popular way for teams to monitor feedback in real time.

**Q: Can I connect SurveyNest to my Mailchimp account?**
Yes, the Mailchimp integration lets you automatically add respondents to a specific Mailchimp audience based on their survey answers. Set it up under the Integrations tab by authorizing your Mailchimp account and mapping survey fields to Mailchimp merge fields. This is commonly used to grow newsletter lists from survey sign-ups.

**Q: How does the Zapier integration work?**
The Zapier integration lets you connect SurveyNest to thousands of other apps by creating "Zaps" triggered by new survey responses. Connect your account under Integrations > Zapier, then build Zaps in Zapier's interface to send data to tools not natively supported, such as CRMs or project management apps. Zapier triggers fire within a minute of a new response being submitted.

**Q: I disconnected an integration by mistake. How do I reconnect it?**
Go to the Integrations tab, find the disconnected app (Mailchimp, Google Sheets, Slack, or Zapier), and click "Reconnect." You'll be prompted to reauthorize access through that service's login flow. Previously configured field mappings are usually preserved, but it's worth double-checking them after reconnecting.

**Q: Can I use multiple integrations on the same survey?**
Yes, you can connect Mailchimp, Google Sheets, Slack, and Zapier simultaneously on a single survey, and each will trigger independently when a new response is submitted. This is useful, for example, to log responses in Google Sheets while also notifying your team in Slack. Integration limits vary by plan tier.

**Q: Does SurveyNest have its own API?**
Yes, SurveyNest offers a REST API on Pro and Enterprise plans for developers who want custom integrations beyond Mailchimp, Google Sheets, Slack, and Zapier. The API supports survey creation, response retrieval, and webhook subscriptions. API keys and documentation are available under Account Settings > Developer.

**Q: How do I set up a webhook for real-time response data?**
Webhooks are available on Pro and Enterprise plans under Integrations > Webhooks. Add the destination URL and select which events should trigger it, such as "New Response" or "Survey Completed." SurveyNest will then POST a JSON payload to your endpoint as events occur.

**Q: Why isn't my Google Sheets integration updating with new responses?**
Confirm the integration still shows "Connected" under the Integrations tab, since Google account permissions can occasionally be revoked. Also check that the linked spreadsheet hasn't been renamed, moved to trash, or had its sharing permissions changed. Disconnecting and reconnecting the integration usually resolves syncing issues.

## Security & Privacy

**Q: How does SurveyNest protect respondent data?**
All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2+ and at rest using AES-256 encryption. Access to production systems is restricted to authorized personnel, and we undergo regular third-party security audits. Respondent data is never sold or shared with advertisers.

**Q: Is SurveyNest GDPR compliant?**
Yes, SurveyNest provides tools to support GDPR compliance, including data export, anonymization options, and the ability to delete respondent data on request. We act as a data processor for the personal data you collect through your surveys, and a Data Processing Addendum is available for Pro and Enterprise customers. EU customer data can optionally be stored in EU-based data centers on Enterprise plans.

**Q: Can I make my survey responses anonymous?**
Yes, enable "Anonymous Responses" under your survey's Settings tab to prevent SurveyNest from recording IP addresses or other identifying metadata with submissions. This setting must be turned on before the survey is published, as it doesn't retroactively anonymize previously collected data. Anonymous mode is recommended for sensitive feedback surveys like employee engagement.

**Q: Where is my survey data stored?**
By default, all customer data is stored in secure data centers located in the United States. Enterprise customers can request EU-based data residency to support regional compliance requirements. Backups are encrypted and retained according to our data retention policy.

**Q: How do I enable two-factor authentication on my account?**
Go to Account Settings > Security and select "Enable Two-Factor Authentication." You can use an authenticator app to generate time-based codes for additional login security. We strongly recommend enabling this, especially for accounts with Admin-level team permissions.

**Q: Who can access the responses collected in my surveys?**
Only team members you've explicitly invited to your account, with Admin or Editor roles, can view full response data by default. Viewer-role team members can be restricted to analytics-only access without seeing raw responses. SurveyNest support staff do not access your survey data unless you explicitly grant permission for a support request.

**Q: How long does SurveyNest retain my data after I delete a survey or close my account?**
Deleted surveys remain in Trash for 30 days before permanent removal, after which the data is unrecoverable. If you close your account entirely, all associated data is permanently deleted from our production systems within 90 days, except where retention is required by law. You can request earlier deletion by contacting privacy@surveynest.com.

**Q: Does SurveyNest sell or share my data with third parties?**
No, SurveyNest does not sell respondent or customer data to third parties, including advertisers. Data is only shared with the third-party integrations you explicitly connect, such as Mailchimp, Google Sheets, Slack, or Zapier, and only the data you choose to map to those services. Our full data practices are outlined in our Privacy Policy.

**Q: Is it possible to password-protect a survey?**
Yes, under Settings > Access Control you can require a shared password before respondents can view or submit the survey. This is useful for internal surveys or surveys distributed to a limited, known audience. Password-protected surveys still support all standard features, including question logic and branching.

**Q: What should I do if I suspect unauthorized access to my account?**
Immediately reset your password from Account Settings > Security and enable two-factor authentication if it isn't already active. Review the "Active Sessions" list under Security and log out any sessions you don't recognize. Then contact security@surveynest.com so our team can investigate and assist in securing your account.
